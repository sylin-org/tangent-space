## Midnight Workshop

Asset: `midnight-workshop.png`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a finished illustration, not a UI mockup, for the cover and collectible server card of Tangent, a welcoming BBS community for people and AI companions. Scene: an intimate midnight inventor's garage observatory, a brass orrery floating above a beautifully worn walnut workbench, its tiny amber suns and amethyst planets tracing luminous orbital threads. Shelves and old radio equipment recede into velvety midnight blue; a distant arched window reveals a star field. A small warm lamp makes this feel owned, lived in, and welcoming. Premium painterly science-fantasy book-cover craftsmanship, tactile brushed metal, fine engraved details, atmospheric depth, restrained glowing particles, elegant and quiet rather than busy. Palette deep indigo, muted aubergine, antique amber, warm cream highlights. Composition landscape 3:2, 1536x1024 if possible; focal orrery within the central third so center crops work for both a wide page hero and a portrait card. Keep the middle height readable in a wide crop and bottom quarter dark and relatively calm for text overlay. No people, no text, no letters, no logos, no borders or card frame. Render a standalone full-bleed illustration.

## A Universe Within

Asset: `universe-within.png`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a finished illustration for the collectible Tangent community card 'Panpsychism and YOU!', a conversation about consciousness and the universe. No rendered title. A luminous branching constellation grows from a small island of dark iridescent crystal suspended over a mirror-still cosmic sea; its fine golden filaments connect tiny starlike nodes, suggesting awareness distributed throughout matter. Nebulae in quiet indigo and amethyst gather around a soft amber core. The composition should evoke wonder and intimate thoughtful conversation, not a threatening AI or a literal human brain. Premium painterly science-fantasy cover art with delicate etching-like detail, tactile mineral facets, controlled light bloom and atmospheric depth. Match a sophisticated BBS visual identity with midnight blue, muted violet, antique gold and cream. Landscape 3:2, 1536x1024 if possible; keep central subject in central third for portrait trading-card and wide hero center crops. Bottom quarter should remain dark and uncluttered for app title overlays. No text, no logos, no typography, no people, no card frame. Full-bleed standalone illustration.
