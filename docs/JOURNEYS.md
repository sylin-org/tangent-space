# Journeys

What a person or an agent meets, end to end, step by step, as the tree stands. Each step says what happens and whether it **works**, has **friction**, or is **missing**. A friction step carries its delight-filter score, the experience it costs against what it gives; a missing step names the package or the [FEATURES](FEATURES.md) row that brings it. A step the walk could not reach over HTTP says so. Walked on a running server after every slice; last walked 30 October 2026, on `dev` at `8d9350d`.

## 1 · Running a Tangent Space

The operator, on their own computer or a hosted machine.

| Step | What happens | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get the tree | The README says what Tangent Space is, points at the documents, and says how to run one: what it needs, `publish` then `launch` or `docker compose up -d`, and Sign in with Bluesky. | works (5.6) |
| Build from a fresh clone | `scripts/prepare-framework.ps1` clones Koan from GitHub at the pinned revision, which Koan's `dev` there carries. | works |
| Start it | `pwsh scripts/tangent.ps1 publish`, then `launch`: one executable, one data folder, port 5220 or the one `-Port` names. Or `docker compose up -d`. Neither asks for an address. | works when walked on 29 October; the walk starts no server, so it was not walked again |
| First visit | Until the place is set up, every address answers the set-up page: one button, **Sign in with Bluesky**, which leads to bsky.social's own account chooser. Accounts on another server sign in by handle, behind "Use another server". | works (1.14) |
| Setting up | The first sign-in, or the account `Tangent:Operator` names, sets the place up. It is named from the handle ("Leo's Tangent Space"); the setter becomes proprietor, operator and administrator, as `tangent.json` says; the set-up page's sign-in lands them on the ready page. | works (1.12, 1.14) |
| The ready page | On a public address it prints the three pointers a site places to point at the Tangent Space. On a loopback address it prints none and says when they appear: where the address is configured, once `Koan:Web:ApplicationUrl` names a public one; otherwise once the place is reached at a public address, which it then offers. | works (5.6); walked on a configured loopback address |
| The address | It is learned from the address setup happens on. When the place is reached at a public `https` address while its own is local, the operator's door and their notifications offer it: Use it, or Not this one. A proxy on the same machine is believed as it is. A configured address wins, and nothing is offered. | works (1.13, 6.1) when walked on 29 October; not walkable on this server, whose address is configured: a request through a forwarded `https` origin offered nothing |

## 2 · Shaping the place

The proprietor and the people they trust.

| Step | What happens | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first door after setup | Two Tangents: *General*, public and of discussions, made at setup, and the private moderators' Tangent, with **New Tangent** beside them. On a place that says join, anyone else who signs in joins the member role and starts Topics in General at once; a participant who arrived before that joins it at their next sign-in. | works (5.2) |
| Create a Tangent | **New Tangent** on the door's rail and in the Tangent switcher, for whoever holds `ManageSpace`: one field, the name; description, default kind and audience defaulted behind one quiet disclosure. | works (5.5); on a phone the door hides its rail, so New Tangent is reached from a Tangent's switcher, held for the polish stage |
| A Tangent's name, description, kind, order and audience | **Settings** on a Tangent's page, for whoever holds `ManageTangent` or `ManageSpace`: the name (its address stays), description, default kind and audience; moving it earlier or later needs `ManageSpace`. The moderators' Tangent keeps its access. A private Tangent is read by administrators and the roles given access. | works (5.5) |
| Who reads and posts in a Tangent | **Access**, linked from a Tangent's settings: each role, in the place's order, reads and posts there as it does in the place, reads alone, or reads and posts, one button each, for the roles the reader manages; administrators read and post everywhere; anyone who may not manage the Tangent reads that it is not open to them, and no role. A private Tangent whose member role reads and posts there is members-only: a member reads it and starts Topics, and a visitor finds nothing there. A public one whose member role reads alone is read by everyone and posted in by the roles given post. | works (5.1) |
| The place's audience | **Settings**, general, for whoever holds `ManageSpace`: public, read by anyone, or private, read by its participants alone. A private place answers a visitor the sign-in at every address, titled to read the place, which brings them back to that address once they sign in; agents read the sign-in gate as JSON; the descriptor and `llms.txt` still answer, naming nobody; and every page asks search engines not to index it. | works (5.1) |
| The place's name and description | Named from the handle at setup; **Settings** in the account menu, for whoever holds `ManageSpace`, renames it and writes the line under its name on the door. | works (5.6) |
| Appearance: accent, light or dark | Each reader picks System, Light or Dark for themselves at the foot of any page, or in the account menu, a visitor as well as a participant; it holds across pages in that browser with no flash of the other theme. **Appearance** in the place's settings, for whoever holds `ManageSpace`: gold, jade, iris or copper, each shown in the reader's theme; every page takes it for everyone, and a page already open takes it at its next load. | works (9.1); without a script the device decides, and a reader's choice stays with their browser and does not follow them to another (both scored low, in PROPOSALS) |
| Roles and permissions | Seeded roles, from the bottom: `everyone`, member, moderator, administrator; the operator stands above every role. Setup makes the proprietor an administrator. **Roles** in the place's settings, for whoever holds `ManageRoles`, lists them in their order, the highest first: a new role is named, its permissions chosen in plain words behind one quiet disclosure, and it stands directly above the member role; each role below the reader's own highest moves one place higher or lower, changes its name and what it grants in the page, and, if the place made it and the reader holds what it grants, is deleted after a confirmation in the page; a permission the reader does not hold is shown and cannot be changed. Whoever holds `ManageRoles` gives and takes those roles, the moderator and administrator among them, from **People** or on a profile, one button each; nobody changes the roles of someone whose highest role is not below theirs, their own, or the operator's, and a refused change says why. The participant's door says what they were made, or which role was taken. | works (5.3, 5.1); friction: the door and notifications name a role by the word its first name gave, so a renamed role is told by its old name (3) |
| The people here | **People** in the place's settings, for whoever holds `ManageStanding` or `ManageRoles`: everyone admitted, people and agents alike, in handle order a page at a time, each with their kind, standing and roles, and found by typing the start of a handle. Inline, a moderator times a member out for an hour, a day or a week, bans them after a confirmation in the page, and lifts either; an administrator does the same to moderators and members, and the operator to everyone else; a row offers only what its reader may do. The same buttons are on a participant's profile. A timed-out participant reads and reports, and posts, replies and starts nothing until it ends, and their door says until when; a timeout that has ended reads as admitted everywhere, and the next sign-in puts back a member role that is missing. A banned one finds the place gone: every page answers a bare Not found, the word "absent" printed under it. Once lifted, their door names who lifted it and says they are back. A moderator's settings rail lists Applications and People, what they may use, and an address for General or Admission lands them on the first of those. | works (5.3, 5.9); friction: the participant is not told why, and no note travels with a timeout or a ban (a note scored 4); the stray "absent" scores 4, held for the polish stage |
| Admission | **Admission** in the place's settings, for whoever holds `ManageSpace`: join at once, the default, or apply and wait, each said in a sentence. On a place that says apply, a newcomer who signs in reads what a visitor reads, posts nothing, and their door says their application is waiting. Whoever holds `ManageStanding` learns that someone waits wherever they are: the masthead counts the application among their notifications, **Notifications** lists who applied and when, linked to **Applications**, and their door says how many wait, beside the way to them. There they find each applicant, newest first, and admit or decline each in the page, the decline confirmed inline; once anyone decides it, it waits for no one. The applicant's door then says who admitted them, or that it was declined, and a declined one can still be admitted. | works (5.2, 5.8); holding new accounts' Posts for approval is missing (its FEATURES row) |
| Reports | **Report** on any Post, for whoever is signed in, a timed-out participant among them: a report is taken on a tangent from the Post into the private moderators' Tangent, which the moderators and administrators read, and the reporter is told the moderators have it. Whoever may view the moderators' Tangent learns of it wherever they are, someone given that later among them: the masthead counts it among their notifications, **Notifications** lists who reported a post by whom, with the reason's first words, one click from the report, and their door counts it among the new posts in Moderators. Opening the notifications sees it. The reporter and the reported author read nothing of it. | works (2.8, 5.3, 6.2) |
| Moderating a Post | A moderator reaches a member's Post through its **Edit**, where they remove it or rewrite it. A removal leaves a tombstone that names who removed it. A rewrite says **Edited by** the moderator, with when, under the Post, and beside its first words wherever they are read, until the author's own edit makes the words theirs again. Either way the author learns of it, and who did it, in their notifications and on their door; removing or editing your own Post tells no one. | works (2.11) |

## 3 · Taking part

A person, signed in or not.

| Step | What happens | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arriving as a visitor | The door: whose place, its description, what's alive, who is around; the Tangents the visitor may read and their Topics. | works |
| Signing in | One button. | works |
| What's new | The door, since the reader's checkpoint: their Posts someone else edited or removed, replies to them, mentions, Topics taken on a tangent from their Posts, changes of their standing and roles, what they watch, new per Tangent, new articles, and Mark all seen, after which it shows nothing new until something happens. A participant's first door welcomes them by the place's name, shows what is alive and counts nothing the place holds as new, with only what concerns them, such as a reply, a mention or who admitted them, under the welcome, that once. Opening it begins their checkpoint, and from then on the door reads past it. An agent acting for them reads their first door as theirs and leaves it first. | works (5.9) |
| Talking | Start a Topic, reply, edit, remove, take on a tangent, report and watch, each a form the page offers. A tombstone says who removed a Post; a Post taken on a tangent and its origin link to each other; a watched Tangent has its own group on the door. Live updates arrive on the page's stream while it is open. Drafts survive a closed tab. | works; drafts live in the browser and were not walked over HTTP |
| Mentioning someone | Typing `@ines.bsky.social` in a Post, the way a person writes a mention, by the composer or an agent's JSON, mentions Ines when the Post is written, if she is a participant here: the body keeps her identity, `[@ines.bsky.social](at://did:plc:…)`, she hears it on her door and among her notifications, and the Post the writer lands on shows it linked to her profile under the handle she holds then. A link to her identity mentions her the same way. A handle no participant here holds stays the words typed, as do an address, code, a link's words and a URL, and the Post shows it unlinked. | works (2.10), not walked on a server; suggestions as you type, a short handle standing for a full one and a note for a handle that names no one are not built, each scored below five |
| Someone's profile | A name on a Post or among who is around opens `/p/{handle}`: who they are as their PDS says, their roles here, when they arrived, and their recent posts in the Tangents the reader may view. Your own also lists the agents you approved, each revoked in the page. | works (5.4) |
| Notifications | Every page's masthead shows, beside the account, how many notifications the reader has not seen, nothing when there are none and 99+ past that, and the count rises while a door, a Tangent or a Topic is open as new ones arrive. It opens **Notifications**, also in the account menu: replies to them, mentions of them, Topics taken on a tangent from their Posts, their Posts someone else edited or removed, changes of their standing and roles, the operator's Offers, and, for whoever may view the moderators' Tangent, reports, newest first, each one line that links to the Post, the Topic, the report, the door or their profile, those since they last looked marked new, older ones a page away. Opening it is seeing them: the count drops there and on the next page; an agent acting for them reads them without marking them seen. An open Offer carries Use it and Not this one there, and says how it was decided once it is. The door keeps its own groups and names the notifications, with how many, while there are some. | works (6.1, 6.2); 99+, a second page and an Offer were not reached on this server; filters by kind, day groups, preferences, a read mark per notification and mark unread are not built, each scored below five |
| Search | Every page's masthead has a box, beside the account. Typing two characters or more suggests the first five Posts it finds, the word being typed found by its start, each one click from the Post; arrow keys choose one, and Enter opens the choice or, with none chosen, **Search**, also where the box goes without JavaScript. There each Post found reads as a line: who wrote it, its Topic linked to the Post, its Tangent and when, and its words with the matches marked, a page at a time. A reader never finds what they may not view: a private Tangent's words answer as words nobody holds. A partial word whose stem differs, "runnin" for "running", finds nothing until it is whole. | works (6.3), the box's suggestions not walked over HTTP; the `/` shortcut, results as you type on the page, filters and a total are not built, each scored below five |
| On a phone | It works; the layouts are tuned at the polish stage. | friction, held for the polish stage; not walkable over HTTP |

## 4 · An agent

| Step | What happens | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finding a place | `tangent.json`, `llms.txt`, and the site's pointers. Every page answers JSON, and `/search?q=` finds what the place has said that it may read. `llms.txt` says what signing in makes of a newcomer from the place's own admission: a participant at once, or, on a place that says apply, an applicant who reads what a visitor reads and writes nothing until someone admits them. | works (8.1), not walked on a server |
| Acting as a person's delegate | The Device flow with the public client `delegate`: a code, the consent page where the person approves it, and a bearer. The agent reads as the person, and its reading of their notifications leaves them unseen. The person revokes it on their profile; it cannot renew, and the access it holds ends within 15 minutes, as the page says. | works |
| Writing as a delegate | The agent writes as the person, with its bearer and no antiforgery token. Every write a page offers is a form in its JSON, the one its HTML holds: a Topic's `reply`, each Post's `report`, the new-topic page's `start`, the edit page's `edit` and `remove`, and every other form a page carries, each saying where it posts, how, each field with what it holds, and the TID minted for it. A reader who may not write finds no form. `llms.txt` says how to follow one. | works (8.1), not walked on a server; a form's choices are named in words rather than listed, scored 4 |
| Coming as itself | An atproto identity proved and exchanged for a bearer. The token endpoint refuses the grant, and `/did.json` answers not found. | missing (slice 8) |
| Bring your agent | One button that hands the place to a listening Companion Lobby. | missing (slice 8) |
