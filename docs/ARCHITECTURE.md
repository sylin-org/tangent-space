# Architecture

The parts of the tree at the root as its code has them: [BRIEF](BRIEF.md) says what the place is for and [LANGUAGE](LANGUAGE.md) what things are called. Each section below is the one place its question is answered, and names the files and members where the code answers it. `$revision` in `scripts/prepare-framework.ps1` pins Koan's source, `global.json` pins the SDK and `src/server/koan.lock.json` records the composed modules by their project names; a Koan package is named here by its id, `Sylin.` and its project's name.

## 1. Nouns

- `TangentSpace` (`src/server/Space/TangentSpace.cs`): the one settings row, keyed `TangentSpace.SingleRow`.
- `Tangent` (`src/server/Tangents/Tangent.cs`): a subject area, keyed by its slug; it counts the Topics it holds, in all, in each month they started and by each author, raised in the commit that starts one or moves one in (`Tangent.TopicCame`) and lowered in the one that moves one out or deletes one (`Tangent.TopicWent`), and names the Topic its moderators pinned (`Tangent.PinnedId`).
- `Post` (`src/server/Posts/Post.cs`): a root Post holds its Topic's title, kind, slug and counters, whether its moderators closed it, by whom and when (`Post.Closed`), and the log sequence of its newest Post (`LastLogSeq`); every Post holds the log sequence of its own arrival (`LogSeq`); a reply names the Post it answers in `Post.ParentId`, `parent` on the wire.
- `Media` (`src/server/Posts/Media.cs`), a Koan `MediaEntity`, and `MediaUse` (`src/server/Posts/MediaUse.cs`), what lets someone see it.
- `FormerAddress` (`src/server/Posts/FormerAddress.cs`): the address a Topic stood at before it moved, keyed `{tangent}/{slug}`, naming its root.
- `Participant` (`src/server/Participants/Participant.cs`): Tangent's facet on the Koan person, keyed by its id; it keeps the slugs of the Tangents its participant quieted (`Participant.Quiet`).
- `Invitation` (`src/server/Participants/Invitation.cs`): a Bluesky account invited by its handle, keyed by its DID, until its holder arrives.
- `Event` (`src/server/Events/Event.cs`), `Checkpoint` (`src/server/Events/Checkpoint.cs`) and `Offer` (`src/server/Offers/Offer.cs`), an Offer's decision.
- `Follow` (`src/server/Events/Follow.cs`): whether a participant follows a Topic, keyed by participant and Topic, with how many Posts someone else wrote there since they caught up with it, which the Post commands compile.

Beside them stand Koan's rows: persons, external identities, sessions, roles (`Role`) and their members (`RoleMember`), overwrites (`PermissionOverwrite`), and media's bytes. `Post`, `TangentSpace` and `Participant` change by methods that validate and throw a `Refusal` and load no other entity, and `Checkpoint`, `Follow` and `Offer` are made by factories that validate nothing (`Checkpoint.At`, `Follow.Of`, `Offer.Decided`); `Tangent` reads other Tangents for a new slug and order, `Event.Append` numbers and saves its row, and `MediaUse.Reaches` reads uses and asks `Capabilities`. No entity opens a transaction.

## 2. One process, one folder

A place runs as one process on its own store (`src/server/Program.cs`), and conversation needs nothing outside it but each participant's own atproto sign-in. The data folder is `Tangent:DataFolder`, `data` beside the executable unless absolute (`TangentSpaceOptions.DataFolder`). It holds the SQLite file `tangent.db` (`TangentSpaceOptions.DatabaseFile`, Koan's connection string in `TangentModule.Register`), Koan's local storage for media, and the data-protection key ring under `keys` (`ParticipantsComposition.KeyFolder`); `RunningMark` marks it in use while the place runs. This store is canonical for conversation and for private content. Backup, restore and archive are verbs of the executable (`LifecycleCommand`), backup through SQLite's `VACUUM INTO`. Unconfigured, the place listens on port 5220 of localhost alone. There is one posture: `TangentSpace.Establish` makes a place public and joined at once, and whoever holds `ManageSpace` changes either. Access protects nothing against the operator's own account or intent, what an admitted participant posts, or the transport.

## 3. A request, as it runs

1. Koan's middleware (`KoanWebStartupFilter`): forwarded headers from trusted proxies, the request's origin noted, the path base, the exception handler, security headers, the refusal of a cross-site write that carries a cookie, static files, the health alias, routing, CORS, authentication and authorization, the rate limiter where one is registered, then the endpoint.
2. Koan's gate (`GateFilter`, ARCH-0146), on every `[Tool]` action and every action that declares its bit with `[Needs]`, asks `IAuthorize`, where the Doorway answers (section 4).
3. The resource filters: `PagePosition.Taken` takes the log's head, then `FirstRunGate`, then `PrivatePlaceGate`; on the door `[LlmsText.Pointer]` runs before all of them.
4. Binding, after which `ToolFilter` refuses a value that cannot bind.
5. The action body, which reads what the Doorway found (`Here.Of`) or judges for itself, sends its command and chooses its answer, or, on an entity controller, Koan's endpoint, which writes the row in one hold with what its hooks write (section 13); the command judges again in its hold, an entity write does not, every Post, Tangent and place row it saves is judged by what every such row holds (section 13), and once it commits, what the hold registered with `AfterCommit` raises the stream's head. On a Post's, its writes and its reads, the controller's override judges what was sent, its own hooks write in the endpoint's hold, its emit composes the answer once that commits, and the endpoint answers a write (`Answers.Written`).
6. `ETagFilter` and the output formatter.

So on a route that declares its bit, who reads and what the address names are judged before anything is bound, and nobody is told to fix what they sent before being told they may not act; on a route that judges for itself, a value is bound, and refused where it cannot bind, first. An address no route names reaches `NotFoundController`, whose catch-all has `Order = int.MaxValue`, through these same steps; an uncaught exception anywhere answers through Tangent's `ProblemExceptionHandler`, which `TangentModule.Register` hands Koan's exception handler.

## 4. Who may read and act here

The Doorway answers it once for every route that declares its bit with `[Needs(bit)]`: `Doorway` (`src/server/Access/Doorway.cs`) is Tangent's `IAuthorizationProvider` on Koan's gate, which runs before anything is bound. `Doorway.Judge` finds the address `{tangent}/{topic}/{post}` as it stands for its reader (`Here.Find`): the Tangent's row, the rows capability is computed from, once, whether the reader may view the place or the Tangent, then the missing resource's answer for a Tangent no row has, a Topic segment that names no Topic by its slug, by its root's TID, as the log names a Topic, or by its former address (`FormerAddress`), and a Post that is not that Topic's, a Topic found by its TID or its former address standing where it stands now, its Tangent's row and rows there, for a reader who may view it, and absent to anyone else; then the bit, on what the address names (`Here.Capabilities`). `AnyOf` names another bit that lets the reader in as well: the people are read by whoever manages standing or roles (`[Needs(Bit.ManageStanding, AnyOf = Bit.ManageRoles)]`), and an image is brought in by whoever may start a Topic or reply. `Place = true` asks the bit of the place where the address names a Tangent, the Tangent still found as it stands for the reader, as deleting a Tangent and downloading it do. A write declares the bit it needs, so someone without it is refused before anything they sent is read, a form with the refusal's page; a page that shows its gate in place for whoever may not use it judges that gate itself, as a Tangent's access does. `[Needs(Bit.View, Participant = true)]` asks for a signed-in participant besides, as a report does. `[Needs(Bit.View, Outside = true)]` lets in a participant for whom the Tangent is absent and who may join or ask to join it — they take part in the place, it is joinable, and their own overwrite does not keep them out (`Inside.Outside`) — with where they stand on the `Here` (`Here.Outside`), every other bit still absent to them: the Tangent page answers them its card, as the list of Tangents shows it, and nothing it holds (`TangentOutside`). `[Needs(Bit.View, Participant = true, Applicant = true)]` lets in a participant a private place is closed to while their application waits or once it was declined, as leaving does, which acts on them alone. A refusal is the one reason `Capabilities.For` names, which Koan answers through `Answers.Refused`; an allow carries the `Here` it found, which Koan puts on the request and the action reads (`Here.Of`), and the Topic page and a Post's answer a root's TID or a former address with a redirect, a `302`, to the same address under the Topic's Tangent and slug. The Doorway defers on a route that declares nothing, on a listing, before first run, and to a visitor of a private place, whom `PrivatePlaceGate` answers. On the Doorway: the door, a profile, the list of Tangents and a new Tangent's page, the Tangent page and quieting it, the Topic and the Post, starting a Topic or taking a Post on a tangent, edit, remove, undoing a removal, a Post's history, report, closing, reopening, pinning, unpinning, following, moving and deleting a Topic and the move's page, publishing and retracting an article, search, a writer's suggestions, reply, bringing an image in, creating a Tangent, a Tangent's settings with their change and deletion, its image, its access with its change and its people with letting in and joining, the place's settings, its admission and its appearance with their changes, its image and its mark, and the appearance preview, export's page and its downloads, applications with admitting, declining, inviting and withdrawing an invitation, people with their standing and role changes, leaving the place and choosing one's kind, roles with their changes, the stream and `change_settings`. Every other route still judges for itself: it reads its participant from the claim (`PostWrites.PersonId`, `ClaimTypes.NameIdentifier`), loads capability with `Capabilities.Load`, `LoadRows` or `LoadEach`, asks `Capabilities.Gate` for one bit and refuses through `Answers.Refused`, as the Connections page does, which sends someone not signed in to sign in and back. Each command loads capabilities again inside its hold (`RemovePost.Run`); a Post's entity write, a Topic started, a reply and an edit, is judged here alone, and its hook reads in its hold only the rows it writes from. Two resource filters answer for every controller of the tree, and Koan's routes and static files pass both. Before first run nothing is open but setting the place up: `FirstRunGate` answers first run unless a controller carries `[AnswersBeforeFirstRun]`, and `Tangent:Operator` may name who sets it up (`TangentSpaceOptions.MaySetUp`). `PrivatePlaceGate` sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on a private place and answers a visitor the sign-in, worded for reading, unless a controller carries `[AnswersEveryVisitor]`.

## 5. `Capabilities.For`

One pure function, computed per request from a few small rows and stored nowhere, returns which bits of `Bit` a reader holds on a resource and one reason for each bit missing, in this order: standing, then `View`, without which every bit says only why it is missing, then the Topic's state and the Post's, a Topic whose root is removed closed to every reply (`RootRemoved`) and one its moderators closed to whoever does not hold `Moderate` there (`Closed`), both saying the word `closed`, then Koan's bag and overwrites resolved in Discord's order on `tangent:{slug}` (`RoleBag.On`, `Overwrites.Resource`), then authorship, asked only of a Post, with a tombstone keeping only `RemoveOwn` as the Post stood, so removing it again answers it, and last what a bit implies: `ManageSpace` every Tangent's `ManageTangent`, and `Moderate` `EditOwn` and `RemoveOwn` on any Post, so every action asks one bit (`src/server/Access/Capabilities.cs`). Standing words `View` for reading (`SignInToRead`, `ApplyToRead`, `DeclinedToRead`) and every other bit for taking part (`SignIn`, `Apply`, `Declined`), and a timeout says until when (`TimedOutUntil`). A public place's audience enters the bag as everyone's `View` (`Capabilities.Resolve`), and on a public Tangent a person's own deny of `View` is left out, so everyone who reads the place reads it, while the row stays and keeps them out again once the Tangent is private (`Capabilities.AsPublic`). A gate the bag and the overwrites decide comes from Koan's deciding fact, as `Capabilities.Denied` or `Capabilities.NoRoleGrants`; `Capabilities.Sentence` and `Capabilities.Heading` word a gate's word for a person. `Capabilities.Can` is the one source of `can`. A change of someone's standing or roles is judged apart, by `People.Judge` and `People.Refusal`, and what standing follows which is the table of section 14. `Roles.Seed` seeds the roles at every start (`TangentModule.Start`); a Tangent's overwrites live on its resource (`src/server/Tangents/Overwrites.cs`).

## 6. Routes and tools

A route is a controller action; a tool is an action with `[Tool(Name = ...)]` and a `[Description]`, which Koan's `ToolAttribute` gives Koan's `GateFilter` and `ToolFilter`, on that action alone (ARCH-0141); an action that declares its bit with `[Needs]`, Tangent's attribute on Koan's typed base `AccessAttribute<Bit>`, gets the gate too (ARCH-0146). The gate asks `IAuthorize` with the action and the route values first: the Doorway judges a declared action, Koan's entity floor defers on an action whose class declares no `[Access]`, and with every provider deferring Koan's `Authorizer` answers its default, allow (`AuthorizeOptions.DefaultDecision`). `ToolFilter` then binds a form or one JSON object alike, and refuses a value that cannot bind, naming its field, or a body past the route's limit, before the body runs; a route without `[Tool]` binds as MVC binds. Koan's start check for a route that declares neither a bit nor `[Open]` (`AuthorizeOptions.RoutesDeclareAccess`) stays off until every route is on the Doorway. The Tangents are Koan's entity controller (`TangentsController`, WEB-0087): declared, so only the operations it overrides are routes and tools — the list (`read_tangents`), the form at `/t/new`, the create (`create_tangent`, `POST /t`), the change (`change_tangent`, `PATCH /t/{tangent}`) and the delete (`delete_tangent`, `DELETE /t/{tangent}`) — each naming what it writes of the row (`[Writes]`) and what it takes beside it (`[Input]`: an audience, a move, a count of Topics), and no caller names a partition; a change leaves a member sent as null as it stands, like one left out (`KoanWebOptions.PartialJsonNulls`, set to `Ignore` for the place), and refuses a default kind or a joining that is not one of its words by its field before anything is held; the controller is the hook of its own requests (`IModelHook`, `IEmitHook`), and its emit composes the page or the JSON, as an action's body does. Its list continues from a sealed `after` in the Tangents' order (DATA-0170), read on past what the reader is not shown so a page is full (`TangentsController.Listing`). Every other route of a Tangent stays a verb beside it. A read of stored rows is a declared Koan `EntityController` (`[Declared]`, WEB-0087), which serves only the operations it overrides, each a route and its tool: the applications (`ApplicationsController`, `read_applications`), a collection over `Participant` continued from one sealed `after` in its `[Pagination]` order, newest application first (DATA-0170), and a profile (`ProfilesController`, `read_profile`), a read by id whose `KeyOf` answers a handle, an id or a DID. Each refuses `?set=`; over HTTP a read ignores a word it does not take and a write refuses one; each takes what it declares beside the row (`[Input]`), narrows its rows in its own options hook and composes its page in its own emit hook, which the formatter draws as any page; the verbs beside them stay on a sibling controller at the same addresses (`ApplicationVerbsController`), since inviting and withdrawing are commands, not writes of a `Participant` row; an applicant is admitted or declined as any standing is given, by `set_standing` (section 14). `tests/Tangent.Tests/Tools/TheToolsTests.cs` holds which actions are tools and which are not. Koan stops the host at start on a tool name that is not lower-case words joined by underscores within 64 characters, or on two tools of one name (`McpToolStartCheck`).

## 7. Refusals

The refusal is Koan's `Reason`: a kind (`Invalid`, `Absent`, `Gated`, `Conflict`), a sentence, its word, which is the kind where it names none, and for an invalid one the wire field it concerns (ARCH-0145, proposed). A command refuses with `Outcome<T>.Refused` or by throwing a `Refusal`, which `Send` turns into one (DATA-0167); the Doorway refuses with `AuthorizeDecision.Refused`. Koan's `Problem` carries status, title, detail, `word` on every refusal and field errors, and no next step. Koan's `Answers` maps a kind to its status once (ARCH-0143): absent `404` saying `Answers.Nothing`, gated `401` to a visitor and `403` otherwise, conflict `409`, invalid `400`, and a command that never ran `503` with `Retry-After`, otherwise `500`; Koan's own gate, where no provider gives a reason, says one of three fixed sentences. Every `401` an action answers carries Tangent's challenge, a page's own such as a private place's sign-in among them: Koan adds `WWW-Authenticate: Tangent proof="…"`, naming the proof door's statement, from `ProofChallenge`, an `IChallenge`. A refusal page and a Problem say the same: `Refused`, the part a refusal page draws, puts the sentence under the heading of its word, and the way to sign in where the word is `sign-in`. Tangent also builds `new Problem(...)` by hand in its controllers (`SeenController`, `TopicsController`). Each rule of a Post's write names the field it concerns on its `Reason` (`Post.NeedsTitle`, `Post.NeedsBody`, `Post.NoParent`), so a JSON refusal names it in `errors` and a refused form shows it at that field (`PostWrites.Concerning`). An entity write whose hold cannot begin behind a busy store answers `500` through `ProblemExceptionHandler`, where a command that never ran answers `503`. An uncaught exception answers a `500` through `ProblemExceptionHandler`, and an unmatched address `404` through `NotFoundController`.

## 8. A tool call as a request

Koan's MCP edge, at `ToolDoor.Path` (`src/server/Space/ToolDoor.cs`), sends each call as a request of the application through Koan.Web's whole pipeline in process, in a scope of its own (`KoanWebPipeline.Send`, ARCH-0144), as the caller of the request in hand: the outer request's headers are carried, the session cookie among them, except those of its connection, its body and its own answer, such as `Host`, `Origin`, `Accept` and the conditionals. So the call meets every step of section 3, and a caller with no cookie is a visitor there as on a page. A value for a route's path or query of the wrong type is refused on its field before any request. Beside the routes the edge offers `read` (`Koan:Mcp:ReadByAddress` in `src/server/appsettings.json`), a GET of any address of the place sent the same way, which goes on through one redirect within the place and refuses another host's address; and every route tool with route values takes an `address` in their place (AI-0042); a reply is sent to its Topic's address and names the Post it answers as `parent` (`PostsController`). A call answers with the status, the body, `Location`, and for `read` the address that answered in `_meta.address`; a refusal as `isError` with the Problem in `structuredContent` and `_meta.problem`, the route's `Retry-After` and `WWW-Authenticate` in `_meta.retryAfter` and `_meta.challenge`, and for a write whether it `landed`. A refusal of the transport is a JSON-RPC error whose `data` is the Problem, and a browser's `Origin` must be the base URL's. The edge keeps no session of its own (`Koan:Mcp:EnableStreamableHttpTransport`, `Exposure` set to `Tools`).

## 9. How an address answers

`HtmlFormatter` is the first output formatter (`TangentModule.Register`): it claims the types `ResourceComponents` registers, each a page record the one page component draws (section 10), and a `Problem` only where the request weighs HTML at least as high as everything else it names, drawn as its page (`RefusalPage`); the `Tangent-Fragment` header (`HtmlFormatter.FragmentHeader`) asks for the page alone, without the shell, and every other answer is Koan's JSON, the record serialized. Components render statically through `HtmlRenderer`, and Koan renders a page's card into its head (`IOpenGraphCardRenderer.RenderHead`). Actions also branch on `PostWrites.WantsJson`, and the door and the Topic page set `Cache-Control` themselves for a signed-in reader, as `HtmlFormatter` does for a refusal page that answers one. `ETagFilter`, a result filter, tags a registered resource whose registration names a version — the door, a Tangent's page, a Topic's and a profile, each the first eight bytes of the SHA-256 of its record as its JSON says it, as a non-negative number (`PageVersion.Of`) — with the representation chosen, and a whole page also with the place row's version, and answers `304` to a tag held. `SentAsSent` keeps a field sent empty as empty. Feeds answer Atom and JSON Feed (`FeedsController`). A Tangent's page reads one bounded page of its Topics, newest first in the Tangent's sort (`TopicList.For`): a discussions Tangent's by last activity, one range of `ix_post_topics`, and an articles Tangent's by when each started, one range of `ix_post_created`, removed ones among them; the door's shelves and an export read each Tangent's list in the same sort. A list is whole or narrowed by `month` to a range of `ix_post_created` or by `author` to one of `ix_post_topics_by`, which ends in the start, each continued through Koan's keyset over the sort, the Id breaking ties (`Keyset<Post>`, `Post.Page`, DATA-0170), from the key of the last Topic a page held, which an address carries sealed for that Tangent's list in that sort alone (`TopicList.Seal`, Koan's `SealedCursor`) and one that does not open is refused naming `after`; the whole list leads its first page with the Topic its moderators pinned, one keyed read on every page of it, which each page leaves out of its own rows, and a narrowed one marks it where it falls (`TopicList.Pinned`); in an articles Tangent its rail offers each month and the authors who started the most (`TopicsController.AuthorLimit`) from the Tangent's own counts, never counted on read, and reads only the people it names. A search (`SearchController`, `PostSearch.Run`) reads Koan's lexical index, best first, through one past the page asked for, in the whole place or, with `in` naming a Tangent the reader may view, told that Tangent alone, and keeps the Posts in Tangents they may view, each Tangent its hits name judged once (`ViewableTangents.Met`), reading the ranking on past a Post they may not view until the page is full and one more is kept, or to the deepest page Koan's index reads (`LexicalQuery.MaximumOffset`); a profile's Posts (`RecentPosts`) and the articles feed (`Feeds.OfArticles`) read the same way through their indexes, each read after the first told to leave out the Tangents met so far that the reader may not view (`ViewableTangents.Met.LeavingOut`), so it fills the page or meets a Tangent no earlier read met, at most `ViewableTangents.Met.ReadLimit` reads: a profile's page that bound ends holds what it kept and continues from the last Post it read, and the feed's window holds what it found. None of them reads a Tangent no row of theirs names. A Tangent's page frames its masthead's box with the search's `in` (`Frame.SearchIn`), and the `search` island carries what the form holds to its suggestions.

## 10. Pages and the frame

A page is a resource record whose properties are parts (section 11), each in a slot of its layout — head, rail or column — by `[Slot]`, in declaration order; a property with no slot is a fact of its JSON alone, and a null part is left out. The record names its layout with `[Page]` — home, a head over a rail and a column; reading; settings, the settings rail beside one panel; panel, one panel in the middle of the frame — or chooses it where it is its own (`ILaidOut`: the new Topic and edit pages write an article in the reading layout and anything else in a panel). One component draws every page (`Page`, `src/server/Render/Page.cs`): `main`, its layout's rail and column, each part through `Draw`, handing every part the reader's id and the UTC day its times are written for; a page that refreshes itself, the door, is the fragment island's host. A slot may hold a part projected from the record's facts, which the JSON leaves out (`[JsonIgnore]`), so the JSON says each fact once. `ResourceComponents.Add<TPage>` registers a page with its title, version, frame and card, and no component.

`Shell.razor` (`src/server/Render/Shell.razor`) draws everything around a page from the `Frame` its registration computes (`src/server/Render/Frame.cs`): the place and its door, the path bar, who is signed in (`Who`, the one record of the reader every page and its JSON carry, built from the Person part (`Who.Of`): their kind, which their mark wears, their own profile, which the account menu opens, and how many things are for them), where a visitor signs in, and the feeds. A page's frame names only its own crumbs, past the thing its address names; `HtmlFormatter` puts before them the Tangent and the Topic the Doorway found for the request (`Here`, `Frame.Under`), the Tangent's crumb opening the switcher of Tangent entries where the page holds one (`Frame.Switcher`), and the place is never a crumb, since the masthead is the root: the door, which names no crumb of its own, has no path bar. Each page record carries the place's name and who reads it, as its controller builds them. A refusal's frame names no reader (`Refused.Frame`); one that turns someone signed in away by who they are, an application waiting or declined or an absence, as a ban answers everywhere, names their account in its panel with the way to sign out (`Refused`). `Standalone`, which export and the archive set, leaves out the scripts, and `Retired` puts the archive's line above the page. The capture host serves a gallery of every part in its states, one page of the home layout from fixed values (`src/server/Capture/Gallery.cs`, `/capture/gallery`).

## 11. Parts

A part is a record that says what a thing on a page is (`IPart`, `src/server/Render/Part.cs`), and each part type has exactly one component in `src/server/Render/Parts`, beside its record, whose `Model` is the part. `Draw` draws any part, or a list of them, by its type; a part that holds another chooses how it is drawn, as one of LANGUAGE's shapes (`Shape`), where that part's component takes one. At start `PartComponents.Check` stops the host on a part type with no component or with two, on a component of the folder that draws no part, and on a page whose slot holds something other than parts or that names no layout. Each part's component writes its own classes, its shape and subject, and a part that holds others sizes them by where they stand; its rules are one block of `src/client/parts.css`, named for it, and the frame and the shapes several parts draw alike are `src/client/frame.css`. No part is a page: a page is a record of these parts, every form among them drawn by `Form`. `scripts/check-parts.ps1` holds it: it fails on Razor outside the parts and the shell, and on a `<form>`, `<input>`, `<select>` or `<textarea>` anywhere but `Form` and an island's root, the article editor's mount and the reply box. The parts:

- **Atoms**, the same wherever one stands: `Person`, a participant as chip, name or row, the one drawing of a person, its card opened from its mark (`Person.PersonCard`) and, as a Post's author, the account the Post was written as; `TangentEntry`, a Tangent as row, tile or, for a members-only one the reader is outside of, card with the way in, its shape where it is given none, which every list builds from the Tangent's row wearing the tile it chose (`TangentEntry.Of`, `Tile.For`), a card without a picture brought in here, which reaches only those who may view the Tangent (`TangentsController.Outside`); `TopicEntry`, a Topic as a row of the table, a card or the lead of a list of articles; `TopicPage.Post`, a Post in its Topic or as a row of its rail; `TangentPage.Edited`, who else edited a Post's words; `Form`, every write (section 12), with `Signed`, who sends it; `Sentence`, what a page says, with its code, stressed words and links, each quoted as written (`Sentence.Plain`); `Code`, code inside a sentence; `LinkButton`, a link drawn as a button; `Icon`, a menu item's icon.
- **Heads and lines**: `PlaceHead`, the door's head, which leaves the place's name to the masthead; `Intro`, its welcome; `FirstSteps`, where its manager makes it theirs; `TangentHead`; `TopicTitle`; `ArticleHead`, read and written alike; `ProfileHead`; `PanelTitle`; `PlaceBand`, the place's band over a panel that speaks for it; `Notice`, a gate's or a refusal's line above a form; `SignInGains` and `Pointing`, what signing in gives and the pointers a site places; `StandingLine`, `Door.Offered`, `Door.Waiting`, `Door.Asking` and `Keeping`, the lines of the door and a profile; `Onward`; `Note`; `ContinueReading`; `LiveUpdates`, the live updater.
- **Containers**: `TangentList`, `Door.Crowd` (who is around), `TopicShelf`, `Door.Shelf`, `Door.Since` and `Door.NewPost` (what is new), `DoorPast.Earlier` (what reached the reader that they have seen), `TopicViews`, `AudienceNote`, `LeaveTangent`, `TangentTopics`, `TopicRail`, `Piece` (an article as its Topic opens), `TopicReplies`, `ReplyBox`, `TopicPage.Origin`, `Connections.Publishing`, `Connections.Elsewhere.Line`, `WrittenList` and `ProfilePage.Written`, `SearchHits` and `SearchPage.Hit`, `TangentDirectory`, `SettingsRail`, `EntryList` and `Entry`, a list of things the reader may act on, each about a part or a sentence with what they may do with it, `Pager`, the pages either side, `QuotedPost`, `AnotherAccount.Offer`, `ArticleEditor`, the article's form in its own layout and an island's root, `Versions`, `TangentBack`, `Specimen`, and `Refused`.

## 12. Forms

## 13. Commands and the log

A command is Koan's `ICommand<T>`, sent in its sender's flow and run under `EntityContext.Hold`, one native transaction, holds taking turns (DATA-0166, DATA-0167); a role change joins it (SEC-0036, `ChangeRole`). A Post is started, replied and edited on Koan's entity endpoint (WEB-0086, WEB-0087, WEB-0088): `TopicsController` (`src/server/Posts/TopicsController.cs`) at `/t/{tangent}` serves the start's form at `new`, the start at `POST ""` and the Topic at `{topic}`, and beside them the Tangent's page at `""` (`read_tangent`), an action of its own rather than the declared collection, because a declared collection continues through the one order its `[Pagination]` names where the routes are compiled (`EntityOperation.Keyset`) and a Tangent's list sorts by its kind, and `PostsController` at `/t/{tangent}/{topic}` the reply at `POST ""` and the Post at `{post}`, read and changed by `PATCH`; each is `[Declared]`, so nothing else of `Post` is a route or a tool and no partition is served, and its address segment is what the Doorway found (`KeyOf`). Each write names the members it writes (`[Writes]`) and what it takes beside them (`[Input]`: a start's cover and origin, a reply's parent, an edit's cover); its override judges the body before the hold (`PostBody.Written`), and the controller, as its own hook, does the rest in the endpoint's hold, opened at the prior read: the Tangent or the root read again, a write sent again answered with the row it made (a short circuit with the prior row, which the endpoint answers as written), another row holding the id refused (`Post.IdTaken`), and the row filled and what follows written (`PostWriting`), which the endpoint then saves. Its author is stamped from the principal (`PostAccess`, Koan's `EntityAccess`). `PostWriting` (`src/server/Posts/PostWriting.cs`) holds what every write that brings or changes a Post does past the door: `Start` and `Reply` fill the row (`Post.StartIn`, `Post.ReplyTo`) and call `Wrote`, which `ReportPost` calls too: it counts a new Topic in its Tangent, appends `post.created` with `Event.Append`, saves the root, records media uses, moves the author's mark and notifies mentions; `Edit` answers an edit that changes nothing, refuses one made from another version as stale (`Post.Stale`), and otherwise snapshots, edits and appends `post.edited`. The capture's seeds and the proofs write Posts past the door through the same steps (`src/server/Capture/Written.cs`). The writes of a Post compile follows in the same hold: the writer of a Topic or a reply follows it unless they stopped (`Follow.Wrote`), and each other follower of a Topic with a new reply has one more Post new to them at the Topic's newest place in the log (`Follow.Moved`), a page of followers at a time through `ix_follow_topics`.  A person's send passes no token, so their words finish, and a Post's entity write runs to its end whoever stops listening; a job reads the network outside any hold, then sends with its own token (`ProfileCapture`). The same Id and request answer the existing Post and another body is a conflict; a write with no Id gets a fresh TID. `Event.Append` takes each field as a parameter, numbers the row from `Event.Log` and registers an `AfterCommit` that tells `EventStream` the head moved (ARCH-0142). Only the writes of a Post keep an event from targeting its own actor (`PostWriting`, `RemovePost`, `Mentions.Notify`); an arrival's `standing.changed` targets its own actor (`FirstArrival`, `Establish`), which the door reads as a first visit. An edit or a removal keeps a copy in `AuditPartition`, and `UndoRemoval` reads a removal's copy back into the row at the same version (`Post.Back`), records its images' uses again as whoever wrote those words, and appends `removal.undone`, which targets no one; who may undo is `UndoRemoval.Gate`: a removal by its author only they, by someone else only whoever holds `Moderate` in its Tangent. `CloseTopic` saves whether a Topic is closed on its root and `PinTopic` the pinned Topic on its Tangent's row, and neither appends to the log: the root says who closed it and when. `MoveTopic` moves a Topic to another Tangent in one hold: its root takes its slug there, or the next numbered suffix (`Slug.Unique`), a `FormerAddress` keeps where it stood, its Posts and their media uses take the new Tangent a page at a time (`Entity.UpsertMany`), and so do its rows of the log, one range of `ix_event_topics`, each also taking where the new Tangent's own rows begin (`Event.Into`), so no reader of the log changes and a row older than the new Tangent is still its own there (`Capabilities.Owns`); the Tangent it leaves unpins it, both Tangents count it where it stands, and `post.moved` is appended last, in the new Tangent, waking the Connections. `DeleteTopic` deletes a Topic whole — its Posts a page at a time with their snapshots, media uses and follows (`DeleteTopic.Rows`) — unpins and stops counting it, and appends `topic.deleted`; it is refused unless the replies it is sent are the replies the root counts (`Post.ReplyCount`, which only rises), and its rows of the log stay, which every reader drops since each needs the Post. Both ask `Moderate` of the Tangent, so a removed root moves and goes too, and both refuse the moderators' Tangent, which keeps its reports. An entity write runs as a command does: Koan's endpoint holds from the prior read to its last after-hook, a thrown `Refusal` rolls it back and answers as a command's, and the controller's hooks write what goes with the row (WEB-0086). `TangentWriting` holds what a Tangent's writes do beside its row, for the controller, first run and the seeds alike: creating one writes its audience as its `everyone` overwrite and appends `tangent.changed`, whose sequence the Tangent keeps (`Tangent.LogSeq`), where its own rows begin, its slug derived from its name (`Tangent.FreeSlug`) and its place after the others (`Tangent.NextOrder`); changing one writes its audience, moves it one place where asked, renumbering the others, moves its image's use and appends `tangent.changed`, and a change of nothing writes nothing; the moderators' Tangent keeps its audience, its place and its joining. Deleting one deletes it whole in one hold — its Posts a page at a time with their snapshots, media uses and follows (`DeleteTopic.Rows`), its asks, every overwrite on its resource (`Overwrites.RemoveAll`), its image's use and its row — and appends `tangent.deleted`; it is refused unless the Topics it is sent are the Topics the Tangent holds, read in the hold, a count moved in the commit that starts, moves or deletes a Topic (`Tangent.TopicCount`), so a bare send deletes only an empty Tangent and one that took a Topic since its settings were read is refused. Its rows of the log stay. What every row of a Post, a Tangent, the place and a participant holds is Koan's entity lifecycle, composed in `TangentModule.Register` (`Post.Holds`, `Tangent.Holds`, `TangentSpace.Holds`, `Participant.Holds`, `BeforeUpsert`), beside the table a participant's standing takes (section 14): it judges each row as it is saved, whatever saves it — a command, a seed, a counter moved, an audit snapshot — throws the `Refusal` the rule names, which `Send` answers and rolls back. A Post's, a Tangent's and the place's rule reads nothing, writes nothing and asks nothing of the row before, so a snapshot of a valid row passes as it is; the participant's standing is judged against the row as it stood (section 14). A Post has a Tangent and an author, a body with no NUL within its kept limit, no title on a reply, a root's title within its limit and a cover only on an article; a Tangent a slug's shape that is not `new`, a name, a description, an accent and an image within their rules; the place a name, a description, an admission, an audience and an appearance within theirs (`Appearance.Broken`, which the appearance preview asks of each part too). What depends on the write stays with it: a TID for a new Post, a judged body, a version. Koan makes each entity's table at start (ARCH-0142), and `TangentModule.TablesExist` makes Tangent's and the audit partition's again; `PostIndex` has Koan rebuild a Post index a restored folder left behind, and `TallyTopics` counts again, at start, the months and authors of a Tangent whose counts do not add up to its Topics.

## 14. A state

A state the place keeps takes one shape, in five points; a participant's standing is the state built in it.

1. **One field on the row it describes,** with what is given with it on the same row, and what the row derives from it compiled as it is saved: `Participant.Standing`, a timeout's end in `StandingUntil`, and `Participant.Holds`, which keeps `StandingUntil`, `AppliedAt` and `WaitingSeq` only where the standing calls for them.
2. **One table of transitions on the entity,** each row a value it goes from and one it goes to, with the word its button carries and the sentence a page confirms it with: `Participant.Standings`.
3. **One rule that refuses any other save,** whoever saves, judged against the row as it stood: Koan's `Transitions` (DATA-0171), declared in `TangentModule.Register` with `People.Word`, so a refusal is a conflict whose word is `transition` and whose sentence names the standings as `to` takes them.
4. **One command that makes a transition and logs one line:** `SetStanding`, judged by `People.Judge` with `Bit.ManageStanding`, appending one `standing.changed`; giving the value in force changes nothing.
5. **One route and its `set_` tool,** whose rows list in `can` the values the table gives from the one in force and whose buttons are drawn from those rows: `POST /settings/people/standing` and `set_standing`, answered through Koan's `Answers` (`Answer`, `Written`, `Kept`), and `PeopleForms.Buttons` on the people, the applications, a profile and the person card, a timeout's button offering its lengths.

The page and the command read the standing in force (`Participant.StandingAt`), where a timeout that has ended reads as admitted, and Koan's rule reads the stored one; the table serves both because every row from admitted is a row from timed out or leaves a stored timeout as it stands.

## 16. What is new

`Unread` (`src/server/Events/Unread.cs`) answers it once, in two units. A row is new to a reader when it reaches them (`Readable`), someone else made it, and it lies past their position: in the log, past `Unread.Floor`, the later of `Participant.ArrivedSeq` and the place mark; in a Topic, past the Topic mark. Each Post carries `LogSeq` and each root `LastLogSeq`, written by the command that writes the Post (`Post.Logged`); `Unread.New` marks a Post new on the Topic page, and `Unread.InTopic` counts a Topic for the Tangent page from its root alone, with no count where the reader holds no mark there. The door reads what is for the reader (`Unread.ForYouNow`) and what is new per Tangent and the front page from one `LogWindow` (`src/server/Events/LogWindow.cs`), and tells every standing change the count counts (`Door.Since`); each group carries every row it kept, at most `Unread.CountLimit`, with one keyed read of the people they name, and its part draws the first `Door.Group.Drawn` and opens the rest in place from one line; its news sentence counts each new Post once and what is for the reader wins: the new articles leave out what is for the reader, and a Tangent's part counts only what nothing for the reader and no new article holds (`Door.InTangent.Others`); for a visitor and a reader with nothing new it adds a shelf for each of the first Tangents that holds a Topic the front page does not already show, led by the Tangent's pinned Topic, one keyed read of it and one bounded `TopicList` read each (`DoorController.Shelves`); every page's masthead counts what is for the reader (`Unread.BadgeFor`), linked to the door, and the `unseen` island raises it from the stream (`src/client/islands/unseen.ts`). A Topic the reader follows is for them while it holds Posts new to them that nothing else for them holds: `Unread.ForYouNow` reads one range of `ix_follow_participants` past the floor, joins its roots and marks to the keyed reads it already makes, and counts what its follow counted since the reader last caught up with it (`Follow.CaughtSeq`), less what of that is already for them, and no more than the Posts past their mark less all that is (`Unread.Followed`); the door lists them as it lists each kind (`Door.Follows`, `Door.Followed`) and leaves their Posts out of each Tangent's count. A Tangent the reader quieted (`Participant.Quiet`, `QuietTangent`) drops out of the window's new Posts on their door, and so from its news, its rail's counts and the front page's marks, while what is for them there stays. With `before`, a signed-in reader's door is paged back (`DoorPast`, a page of its own with no live part): `Unread.Earlier` reads the same three ranges before that sequence, `Unread.OlderLimit` and one more each, keeps what lies past the last row of the fullest, so `next` is complete, leaves out what is still new, saying whether it left any out (`Unread.Past.StillNew`), keeps a removal only while its Post stays removed and only the one that removed it (`Post.RemovedAt`), and holds the newest `Unread.OlderLimit` of what is left; the door's news names where it starts, past what it read through (`Door.Since.Older`). A role's mention is one row that names the role (`Mentions.Notify`), which `Unread.ForYouNow` reads for each holder from one range of `ix_event_roles`, as a report is one row read by whoever may view the moderators' Tangent; a Post that reaches the reader as a reply, a Topic taken from theirs or a report is one thing for them, whatever it mentions. An entry that brought a Post (`Unread.Arrival`) lasts while that Post is new; an edit, a removal or a mention an edit added is judged by the log position alone, a removal holding once for each Post and only while it stays removed, so an undone one leaves. No read writes. `ReportPost` starts and replies through `PostWriting.Wrote`, as a Topic started and a reply do.

## 17. `Readable`

`Readable` (`src/server/Events/Readable.cs`) says whether one row of the log reaches one reader: a row in a Tangent reaches whoever may view that Tangent, where the row is one of its own (`Capabilities.Owns`: written since `Tangent.LogSeq`, or taken into it by a move, `Event.Into`), and a let reaches the participant it names while its Tangent stands; a Tangent a row names that has no row of its own exists for no one (`Capabilities.LoadEach`), so a deleted Tangent's rows reach no one, on the door as in a poll or a stream, and none reaches the readers of a Tangent that takes its slug later; a row in no Tangent reaches a reader where it changed the place, names them as its target, or is an application and they hold `ManageStanding`. A stream reads what its reader holds again after `Readable.Refresh`, a second, and at once on a row that changes a standing, a role, a Tangent or the place, or deletes a Tangent. `Readable.Newest` scans at most `Readable.ScanLimit` rows for where a reader begins.

## 22. Sign-in and the profile

A participant is the place's own person, bound to one Bluesky account: the server's build composes one sign-in connector, Koan's atproto connector, and Koan binds the verified login to a person of its own, a GUID v7 the first time the login is seen (SEC-0040). `ParticipantSignIn`, at priority 1100, sends `FirstArrival` or `RecordSignIn` with the session's `atproto:did` and handle, which bind the participant to that account (`Participant.Did`) and, for another one, drop the profile cached for the earlier; `FirstRunSignIn`, at 1200, matches `Tangent:Operator` against that DID or handle and establishes the place. The profile is read and never edited here. A participant chooses on their own profile to be shown as an agent and takes it back (`ChooseKind`, `Participant.Choose`, `PeopleController.Choose` at `/kind`, the `choose_kind` tool), which marks the row `KindSource.OwnChoice`; the label wins: a capture whose label says `bot` makes the row an agent by `KindSource.ProfileLabel`, and taking that back is refused (`Participant.Labelled`), so existing rows need nothing. An `Invitation` names an account by its handle where admission says apply (`ApplicationVerbsController.Invite`, `ProfileReader.Find`, in the request and outside any command, then `Invite`); `FirstArrival` reads it in its hold, arrives the account admitted and removes it, which covers the proof door too, and naming someone who already applied admits them as `SetStanding` does. Their Posts, the log's rows, their Connection's receipts and the media uses they brought stay, naming an id no one holds again, and a later sign-in with the same account arrives anew; an id that reaches no participant reads as someone who left (`Person.Left`). [ADR 0010](adr/0010-local-profile-capture.md) records the local profile cache.

Nothing else reads a participant's kind, so nothing behaves differently for an agent.

## 25. Connections

A participant's `Connection` (`src/server/Connections/Connection.cs`), keyed by their id, is a Koan job that publishes what they wrote under the atproto account they are bound to; an article written as an earlier account stays with it, its copies where their receipts' `at://` addresses say (`Receipt.Repository`, `ArticleState.Earlier`): one that should not be out is taken down there through that account's grant while it holds (`AtprotoAdapter.MayChange`), and is said to stay where the grant does not hold (`Receipt.Stays`); the place's publication under an earlier account of its proprietor is withdrawn the same way. The first time they choose to publish a public article (`PublishController`, the `publish` tool), the place asks atproto for the write grant, for their account alone (`AtprotoChallenge.ForAccount`), never at sign-in; a grant not given comes back to the Connections page, which says why, and a JSON or tool caller whose request cannot start is refused with `not-granted`; then `ChooseToPublish` starts the Connection at the log's head and the article waits as a `Receipt` at version zero. From there their new public articles go out on their own (`Connection.From`, `Connection.OnItsOwn`) until they stop that (`StopPublishing`, the `stop_publishing` tool), after which each goes out once they choose it and `PublishOnTheirOwn` (`resume_publishing`) starts again at the log's head; the grant stays either way, and either tool answers with the first page of their Connections, as `read_connections` reads it. The author takes one article's copy down while it stays here (`PublishController.Retract`, the `retract` tool): `Withhold` marks its receipt (`Receipt.Withheld`), the run takes the copy down, and nothing of it goes out again until they publish it again, which starts a fresh receipt. Every commit wakes the Connections that publish (`Nudge`, from `Event.Append`'s `AfterCommit`), read a page at a time by Id, and none at all once a wake found none until a Connection changes; each reads the log from its own cursor and compares each of its participant's articles with its receipt (`Syndication`): one ahead of its receipt — a later version, or moved to another Tangent (`Receipt.Behind`) — and public is published or updated, so its copy names where it stands, one removed or private is retracted, the copy of one deleted, alone or with its Tangent, is taken down from its receipt alone (`Syndication.TakeDown`), and `KeepReceipt` records what it did and the Tangent the article stands in; a Tangent's settings count the copies still out of its articles from `ix_receipt_tangents` (`Receipt.Out`) for whoever may delete it. Only the author's own words go out as theirs: a version someone else edited (`Post.EditedBy`) waits until they choose it (`Receipt.Chosen`, `Receipt.Theirs`). A failure leaves the cursor where it was, says why on the Connection (`Connection.Trouble`) and on the article, and the run tries again later, half a minute after the first failure and twice as long after each since, at most an hour (`Connection.Failures`, `Connection.RetryAt`); a refusal that needs the grant waits for it. A run that read a full page of the log keeps its cursor and runs again at once for the rest. Both lean on Koan keeping what a job's run changed on its Connection when it asks to run again later (Koan's JOBS-0011). `AtprotoAdapter` writes through Koan's `IAtprotoRepository`: the article as `site.standard.document`, keyed by the Post's TID, its cover as a blob, its page as canonical, and a Bluesky post under the same key that embeds it; and, from the proprietor's Connection, the place as their `site.standard.publication` (`Publication`), which `/.well-known/site.standard.publication` names while the place is public: its address, name and description; its mark, where it is a picture, as its `icon`, cut square as one frame of PNG and sent as a blob only for a mark or an account the record has not carried (`AtprotoAdapter.Icon`, `Publication.Mark`, `Publication.Icon`); and, under Tangent's palette, its first four tokens as Paper draws them, as its `basicTheme`, read from the stylesheets the place serves (`FirstFour`), none under a base's own palette; put again on the proprietor's next run whenever what it says changes (`AtprotoAdapter.Fingerprint`); the Connection that put it withdraws it once the place is private or someone else is its proprietor. An article's page carries `<link rel="site.standard.document">` once it is out, and tells its author where it stands (`Publishing`, the `Connections.Publishing` part), one going out on its own reading as publishing, or its trouble, while the Connection has yet to read the article's arrival, and one it read past without sending offering the publish; their Connections page lists every article (`ConnectionsController`), a page at a time from a cursor over `ix_receipt_connections` (`Receipt.Of`), each in the same word as its page and, while its copy is out, with the press that takes it down and comes back to the first page, which the row then heads; a copy still out of an article that should not be, private here, removed or deleted, is said to remain, with what takes it down (`ArticleState.Remains`), one deleted from its receipt alone, and such an article that never went out is held (`ArticleState.Held`). What the network says about a published article comes back as attributed references (`Elsewhere`), never as Posts: once it goes out, a Koan job per article reads its Bluesky post's counts, replies and quotes from the public AppView (`AppView`, at `Tangent:Profile:BaseAddress`), keeps the counts and the newest replies and quotes, but none its author hid there — a reply its post's gate lists (`hiddenReplies`), a quote they detached — nor any post Bluesky labels, or whose account it labels, to hide or take down (`!hide`, `!takedown`), nor any of an account that asks apps to show it to no one signed out (`!no-unauthenticated`), for every reader alike, and submits its next run as a job of its own, since a poll outlives a job's deadline — every two minutes while someone read the page lately, which a page read tells only in memory (`Readers`), doubling to an hour while no one has; a retracted copy, or one whose article is deleted, forgets them and stops. The article's page shows them under it for everyone (the `Connections.Elsewhere.Line` part), and its JSON carries them.

## 26. Tests

`tests/Tangent.Tests/TangentSpaceFixture.cs` starts the application's own composition on Kestrel at a free loopback port over a new temp folder. The folders of `tests/Tangent.Tests` are some named for a `src/server` folder (`Access/`, `Events/`, `Posts/`) and some not (`Writes/`, `Door/`, `Live/`, `Topics/`). Tests run in parallel except the `ParseCount` collection (`tests/Tangent.Tests/Markdown/RenderOnceTests.cs`). `TheToolsTests` holds what is a tool. `scripts/check-rules.ps1` runs the rule checks — language, greenfield, no dialogs, ports, and parts, which refuses a `.razor` file outside `src/server/Render/Parts` and the shell — and `tests/rules` holds the no-dialogs check. The client's own tests are under `src/client/tests`. The capture build compiles `src/server/Capture/` only when `TangentCaptureHost` is `true` (`src/server/Tangent.Server.csproj`), driven from `src/client/capture/`.
