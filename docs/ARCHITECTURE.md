# Architecture

The parts of the tree at the root as its code has them: [BRIEF](BRIEF.md) says what the place is for and [LANGUAGE](LANGUAGE.md) what things are called. Each section below is the one place its question is answered, and names the files and members where the code answers it. `$revision` in `scripts/prepare-framework.ps1` pins Koan's source, `global.json` pins the SDK and `src/server/koan.lock.json` records the composed modules by their project names; a Koan package is named here by its id, `Sylin.` and its project's name.

## 1. Nouns

- `TangentSpace` (`src/server/Space/TangentSpace.cs`): the one settings row, keyed `TangentSpace.SingleRow`.
- `Tangent` (`src/server/Tangents/Tangent.cs`): a subject area, keyed by its slug; it counts the Topics it holds, in all, in each month they started and by each author, raised in the commit that starts one or moves one in (`Tangent.TopicCame`) and lowered in the one that moves one out or deletes one (`Tangent.TopicWent`), and names the Topic its moderators pinned (`Tangent.PinnedId`).
- `Post` (`src/server/Posts/Post.cs`): a root Post holds its Topic's title, kind, slug and counters, whether its moderators closed it, by whom and when (`Post.Closed`), and the log sequence of its newest Post (`LastLogSeq`); every Post holds the log sequence of its own arrival (`LogSeq`); a reply names the Post it answers in `Post.ParentId`, `parent` on the wire.
- `Media` (`src/server/Posts/Media.cs`), a Koan `MediaEntity`, and `MediaUse` (`src/server/Posts/MediaUse.cs`), what lets someone see it.
- `FormerAddress` (`src/server/Posts/FormerAddress.cs`): the address a Topic stood at before it moved, keyed `{tangent}/{slug}`, naming its root.
- `Participant` (`src/server/Participants/Participant.cs`): Tangent's facet on the Koan person, keyed by its id; it keeps the slugs of the Tangents its participant quieted (`Participant.Quiet`).
- `Invitation` (`src/server/Participants/Invitation.cs`): a Bluesky account invited by its handle, keyed by its DID, until its holder arrives.
- `Event` (`src/server/Events/Event.cs`), `Checkpoint` (`src/server/Events/Checkpoint.cs`) and `Offer` (`src/server/Offers/Offer.cs`), an Offer's decision.
- `Follow` (`src/server/Events/Follow.cs`): whether a participant follows a Topic, keyed by participant and Topic, with how many Posts someone else wrote there since they caught up with it, which the Post commands compile.

Beside them stand Koan's rows: persons, external identities, sessions, roles (`Role`) and their members (`RoleMember`), overwrites (`PermissionOverwrite`), and media's bytes. `Post`, `TangentSpace` and `Participant` change by methods that validate and throw a `Refusal` and load no other entity, and `Checkpoint`, `Follow` and `Offer` are made by factories that validate nothing (`Checkpoint.At`, `Follow.Of`, `Offer.Decided`); `Tangent` reads other Tangents for a new slug and order, `Event.Append` numbers and saves its row, and `MediaUse.Reaches` reads uses and asks `Capabilities`. No entity opens a transaction.

## 2. One process, one folder

A place runs as one process on its own store (`src/server/Program.cs`), and conversation needs nothing outside it but each participant's own atproto sign-in. The data folder is `Tangent:DataFolder`, `data` beside the executable unless absolute (`TangentSpaceOptions.DataFolder`). It holds the SQLite file `tangent.db` (`TangentSpaceOptions.DatabaseFile`, Koan's connection string in `TangentModule.Register`), Koan's local storage for media, and the data-protection key ring under `keys` (`ParticipantsComposition.KeyFolder`); `RunningMark` marks it in use while the place runs. This store is canonical for conversation and for private content. Backup, restore and archive are verbs of the executable (`LifecycleCommand`), backup through SQLite's `VACUUM INTO`. Unconfigured, the place listens on port 5220 of localhost alone. There is one posture: `TangentSpace.Establish` makes a place public and joined at once, whoever holds `ManageSpace` changes either, and `NewAccountsAfterApproval` is stored and read by nothing. Access protects nothing against the operator's own account or intent, what an admitted participant posts, or the transport.

## 3. A request, as it runs

1. Koan's middleware (`KoanWebStartupFilter`): forwarded headers from trusted proxies, the request's origin noted, the path base, the exception handler, security headers, the refusal of a cross-site write that carries a cookie, static files, the health alias, routing, CORS, authentication and authorization, the rate limiter where one is registered, then the endpoint.
2. Koan's gate (`GateFilter`, ARCH-0146), on every `[Tool]` action and every action that declares its bit with `[Needs]`, asks `IAuthorize`, where the Doorway answers (section 4).
3. The resource filters: `PagePosition.Taken` takes the log's head, then `FirstRunGate`, then `PrivatePlaceGate`; on the door `[LlmsText.Pointer]` runs before all of them.
4. Binding, after which `ToolFilter` refuses a value that cannot bind.
5. The action body, which reads what the Doorway found (`Here.Of`) or judges for itself, sends its command and chooses its answer; the command judges again in its hold, and once it commits, what the hold registered with `AfterCommit` raises the stream's head.
6. `ETagFilter` and the output formatter.

So on a route that declares its bit, who reads and what the address names are judged before anything is bound, and nobody is told to fix what they sent before being told they may not act; on a route that judges for itself, a value is bound, and refused where it cannot bind, first. An address no route names reaches `NotFoundController`, whose catch-all has `Order = int.MaxValue`, through these same steps; an uncaught exception anywhere answers through Tangent's `ProblemExceptionHandler`, which `TangentModule.Register` hands Koan's exception handler.

## 4. Who may read and act here

The Doorway answers it once for every route that declares its bit with `[Needs(bit)]`: `Doorway` (`src/server/Access/Doorway.cs`) is Tangent's `IAuthorizationProvider` on Koan's gate, which runs before anything is bound. `Doorway.Judge` finds the address `{tangent}/{topic}/{post}` as it stands for its reader (`Here.Find`): the Tangent's row, the rows capability is computed from, once, whether the reader may view the place or the Tangent, then the missing resource's answer for a Tangent no row has, a Topic segment that names no Topic by its slug, by its root's TID, as the log names a Topic, or by its former address (`FormerAddress`), and a Post that is not that Topic's, a Topic found by its TID or its former address standing where it stands now, its Tangent's row and rows there, for a reader who may view it, and absent to anyone else; then the bit, on what the address names (`Here.Capabilities`). `[Needs(Bit.View, Participant = true)]` asks for a signed-in participant besides, as a report does. `[Needs(Bit.View, Outside = true)]` lets in a participant for whom the Tangent is absent and who may join or ask to join it — they take part in the place, it is joinable, and their own overwrite does not keep them out (`Inside.Outside`) — with where they stand on the `Here` (`Here.Outside`), every other bit still absent to them: the Tangent page answers them its card, as the list of Tangents shows it, and nothing it holds (`TangentOutside`). A refusal is the one reason `Capabilities.For` names, which Koan answers through `Answers.Refused`; an allow carries the `Here` it found, which Koan puts on the request and the action reads (`Here.Of`), and the Topic page and a Post's answer a root's TID or a former address with a redirect, a `302`, to the same address under the Topic's Tangent and slug. The Doorway defers on a route that declares nothing, on a listing, before first run, and to a visitor of a private place, whom `PrivatePlaceGate` answers. On the Doorway: the door, a profile, the list of Tangents and a new Tangent's page, the Tangent page, the Topic and the Post, starting a Topic or taking a Post on a tangent, edit, remove, undoing a removal, report, closing, reopening, pinning, unpinning, moving and deleting a Topic and the move's page, search, reply, a Tangent's settings and access, the place's settings, its admission and its appearance with their changes and the appearance preview, export's page, applications, people, roles and `change_settings`. Every other route still judges for itself: it reads its participant from the claim (`PostWrites.PersonId`, `ClaimTypes.NameIdentifier`), loads capability with `Capabilities.Load`, `LoadRows` or `LoadEach`, asks `Capabilities.Gate` for one bit and refuses through `Answers.Refused`; those under a Tangent find their address with `Here.Find` (`NewMediaController`). Each command loads capabilities again inside its hold (`Reply.Run`). Two resource filters answer for every controller of the tree, and Koan's routes and static files pass both. Before first run nothing is open but setting the place up: `FirstRunGate` answers first run unless a controller carries `[AnswersBeforeFirstRun]`, and `Tangent:Operator` may name who sets it up (`TangentSpaceOptions.MaySetUp`). `PrivatePlaceGate` sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on a private place and answers a visitor the sign-in, worded for reading, unless a controller carries `[AnswersEveryVisitor]`.

## 5. `Capabilities.For`

One pure function, computed per request from a few small rows and stored nowhere, returns which bits of `Bit` a reader holds on a resource and one reason for each bit missing, in this order: standing, then `View`, without which every bit says only why it is missing, then the Topic's state and the Post's, a Topic whose root is removed closed to every reply (`RootRemoved`) and one its moderators closed to whoever does not hold `Moderate` there (`Closed`), both saying the word `closed`, then Koan's bag and overwrites resolved in Discord's order on `tangent:{slug}` (`RoleBag.On`, `Overwrites.Resource`), then authorship, asked only of a Post, with a tombstone keeping only `RemoveOwn` as the Post stood, so removing it again answers it, and last what a bit implies: `ManageSpace` every Tangent's `ManageTangent`, and `Moderate` `EditOwn` and `RemoveOwn` on any Post, so every action asks one bit (`src/server/Access/Capabilities.cs`). Standing words `View` for reading (`SignInToRead`, `ApplyToRead`, `DeclinedToRead`) and every other bit for taking part (`SignIn`, `Apply`, `Declined`), and a timeout says until when (`TimedOutUntil`). A public place's audience enters the bag as everyone's `View` (`Capabilities.Resolve`), and on a public Tangent a person's own deny of `View` is left out, so everyone who reads the place reads it, while the row stays and keeps them out again once the Tangent is private (`Capabilities.AsPublic`). A gate the bag and the overwrites decide comes from Koan's deciding fact, as `Capabilities.Denied` or `Capabilities.NoRoleGrants`; `Capabilities.Sentence` and `Capabilities.Heading` word a gate's word for a person. `Capabilities.Can` is the one source of `can`. A change of someone's standing or roles is judged apart, by `People.Judge` and `People.Refusal`. `Roles.Seed` seeds the roles at every start (`TangentModule.Start`); a Tangent's overwrites live on its resource (`src/server/Tangents/Overwrites.cs`).

## 6. Routes and tools

A route is a controller action; a tool is an action with `[Tool(Name = ...)]` and a `[Description]`, which Koan's `ToolAttribute` gives Koan's `GateFilter` and `ToolFilter`, on that action alone (ARCH-0141); an action that declares its bit with `[Needs]`, Tangent's attribute on Koan's typed base `AccessAttribute<Bit>`, gets the gate too (ARCH-0146). The gate asks `IAuthorize` with the action and the route values first: the Doorway judges a declared action, Koan's entity floor defers on an action whose class declares no `[Access]`, and with every provider deferring Koan's `Authorizer` answers its default, allow (`AuthorizeOptions.DefaultDecision`). `ToolFilter` then binds a form or one JSON object alike, and refuses a value that cannot bind, naming its field, or a body past the route's limit, before the body runs; a route without `[Tool]` binds as MVC binds. Koan's start check for a route that declares neither a bit nor `[Open]` (`AuthorizeOptions.RoutesDeclareAccess`) stays off until every route is on the Doorway. `tests/Tangent.Tests/Tools/TheToolsTests.cs` holds which actions are tools and which are not. Koan stops the host at start on a tool name that is not lower-case words joined by underscores within 64 characters, or on two tools of one name (`McpToolStartCheck`).

## 7. Refusals

The refusal is Koan's `Reason`: a kind (`Invalid`, `Absent`, `Gated`, `Conflict`), a sentence, its word, which is the kind where it names none, and for an invalid one the wire field it concerns (ARCH-0145, proposed). A command refuses with `Outcome<T>.Refused` or by throwing a `Refusal`, which `Send` turns into one (DATA-0167); the Doorway refuses with `AuthorizeDecision.Refused`. Koan's `Problem` carries status, title, detail, `word` on every refusal and field errors, and no next step. Koan's `Answers` maps a kind to its status once (ARCH-0143): absent `404` saying `Answers.Nothing`, gated `401` to a visitor and `403` otherwise, conflict `409`, invalid `400`, and a command that never ran `503` with `Retry-After`, otherwise `500`; Koan's own gate, where no provider gives a reason, says one of three fixed sentences. Every `401` an action answers carries Tangent's challenge, a page's own such as a private place's sign-in among them: Koan adds `WWW-Authenticate: Tangent proof="…"`, naming the proof door's statement, from `ProofChallenge`, an `IChallenge`. A refusal page and a Problem say the same: `Refused`, the part a refusal page draws, puts the sentence under the heading of its word, and the way to sign in where the word is `sign-in`. Tangent also builds `new Problem(...)` by hand in its controllers (`SeenController`, `TopicPageController`), and keeps which reason of a Post's write concerns which field in `PostWrites.FieldOf`, by which a form shows it at that field (`PostWrites.Concerning`) and a JSON refusal names it in `errors` (`PostWrites.Refused`). An uncaught exception answers a `500` through `ProblemExceptionHandler`, and an unmatched address `404` through `NotFoundController`.

## 8. A tool call as a request

Koan's MCP edge, at `ToolDoor.Path` (`src/server/Space/ToolDoor.cs`), sends each call as a request of the application through Koan.Web's whole pipeline in process, in a scope of its own (`KoanWebPipeline.Send`, ARCH-0144), as the caller of the request in hand: the outer request's headers are carried, the session cookie among them, except those of its connection, its body and its own answer, such as `Host`, `Origin`, `Accept` and the conditionals. So the call meets every step of section 3, and a caller with no cookie is a visitor there as on a page. A value for a route's path or query of the wrong type is refused on its field before any request. Beside the routes the edge offers `read` (`Koan:Mcp:ReadByAddress` in `src/server/appsettings.json`), a GET of any address of the place sent the same way, which goes on through one redirect within the place and refuses another host's address; and every route tool with route values takes an `address` in their place (AI-0042); a reply's route ends in an optional Post (`ReplyController`), so a Post's address, which every write answers with, replies to that Post. A call answers with the status, the body, `Location`, and for `read` the address that answered in `_meta.address`; a refusal as `isError` with the Problem in `structuredContent` and `_meta.problem`, the route's `Retry-After` and `WWW-Authenticate` in `_meta.retryAfter` and `_meta.challenge`, and for a write whether it `landed`. A refusal of the transport is a JSON-RPC error whose `data` is the Problem, and a browser's `Origin` must be the base URL's. The edge keeps no session of its own (`Koan:Mcp:EnableStreamableHttpTransport`, `Exposure` set to `Tools`).

## 9. How an address answers

`HtmlFormatter` is the first output formatter (`TangentModule.Register`): it claims the types `ResourceComponents` registers, each a page record the one page component draws (section 10), and a `Problem` only where the request weighs HTML at least as high as everything else it names, drawn as its page (`RefusalPage`); the `Tangent-Fragment` header (`HtmlFormatter.FragmentHeader`) asks for the page alone, without the shell, and every other answer is Koan's JSON, the record serialized. Components render statically through `HtmlRenderer`, and Koan renders a page's card into its head (`IOpenGraphCardRenderer.RenderHead`). Actions also branch on `PostWrites.WantsJson`, and the door and the Topic page set `Cache-Control` themselves for a signed-in reader, as `HtmlFormatter` does for a refusal page that answers one. `ETagFilter`, a result filter, tags a registered resource whose registration names a version — the door, a Tangent's page, a Topic's and a profile, each the first eight bytes of the SHA-256 of its record as its JSON says it, as a non-negative number (`PageVersion.Of`) — with the representation chosen, and a whole page also with the place row's version, and answers `304` to a tag held. `SentAsSent` keeps a field sent empty as empty. Feeds answer Atom and JSON Feed (`FeedsController`). A Tangent's page reads one bounded page of its Topics, newest first in the Tangent's sort (`TopicList.For`): a discussions Tangent's by last activity, one range of `ix_post_topics`, and an articles Tangent's by when each started, one range of `ix_post_created`, removed ones among them; the door's shelves and an export read each Tangent's list in the same sort. A list is whole or narrowed by `month` to a range of `ix_post_created` or by `author` to one of `ix_post_topics_by`, which ends in the start, each continued by the same cursor, which names the last Topic by its place in the sort; the whole list leads its first page with the Topic its moderators pinned, one keyed read on every page of it, which each page leaves out of its own rows, and a narrowed one marks it where it falls (`TopicList.Pinned`); in an articles Tangent its rail offers each month and the authors who started the most (`TangentPageController.AuthorLimit`) from the Tangent's own counts, never counted on read, and reads only the people it names. A search (`SearchController`, `PostSearch.Run`) asks Koan's lexical index for one page of the Posts the reader may view: in the whole place, told the Tangents they may view or the others, whichever are fewer (`ViewableTangents.All`), or, with `in` naming a Tangent they may view, told that Tangent alone, which costs one keyed read of the Tangent and one of what they hold there; a Tangent's page frames its masthead's box with that `in` (`Frame.SearchIn`), and the `search` island carries what the form holds to its suggestions.

## 10. Pages and the frame

A page is a resource record whose properties are parts (section 11), each in a slot of its layout — head, rail or column — by `[Slot]`, in declaration order; a property with no slot is a fact of its JSON alone, and a null part is left out. The record names its layout with `[Page]` — home, a head over a rail and a column; reading; settings, the settings rail beside one panel; panel, one panel in the middle of the frame — or chooses it where it is its own (`ILaidOut`: the new Topic and edit pages write an article in the reading layout and anything else in a panel). One component draws every page (`Page`, `src/server/Render/Page.cs`): `main`, its layout's rail and column, each part through `Draw`, handing every part the reader's id and the UTC day its times are written for; a page that refreshes itself, the door, is the fragment island's host. A slot may hold a part projected from the record's facts, which the JSON leaves out (`[JsonIgnore]`), so the JSON says each fact once. `ResourceComponents.Add<TPage>` registers a page with its title, version, frame and card, and no component.

`Shell.razor` (`src/server/Render/Shell.razor`) draws everything around a page from the `Frame` its registration computes (`src/server/Render/Frame.cs`): the place and its door, the path bar, who is signed in (`Who`, the one record of the reader every page and its JSON carry, built from the Person part (`Who.Of`): their kind, which their mark wears, their own profile, which the account menu opens, and how many things are for them), where a visitor signs in, and the feeds. A page's frame names only its own crumbs, past the thing its address names; `HtmlFormatter` puts before them the Tangent and the Topic the Doorway found for the request (`Here`, `Frame.Under`), the Tangent's crumb opening the switcher of Tangent entries where the page holds one (`Frame.Switcher`), and the place is never a crumb, since the masthead is the root: the door, which names no crumb of its own, has no path bar. Each page record carries the place's name and who reads it, as its controller builds them. A refusal's frame names no reader (`Refused.Frame`); one that turns someone signed in away by who they are, an application waiting or declined or an absence, as a ban answers everywhere, names their account in its panel with the way to sign out (`Refused`). `Standalone`, which export and the archive set, leaves out the scripts, and `Retired` puts the archive's line above the page. The capture host serves a gallery of every part in its states, one page of the home layout from fixed values (`src/server/Capture/Gallery.cs`, `/capture/gallery`).

## 11. Parts

A part is a record that says what a thing on a page is (`IPart`, `src/server/Render/Part.cs`), and each part type has exactly one component in `src/server/Render/Parts`, beside its record, whose `Model` is the part. `Draw` draws any part, or a list of them, by its type; a part that holds another chooses how it is drawn, as one of LANGUAGE's shapes (`Shape`), where that part's component takes one. At start `PartComponents.Check` stops the host on a part type with no component or with two, on a component of the folder that draws no part, and on a page whose slot holds something other than parts or that names no layout. Each part's component writes its own classes, its shape and subject, and a part that holds others sizes them by where they stand; its rules are one block of `src/client/parts.css`, named for it, and the frame and the shapes several parts draw alike are `src/client/frame.css`. No part is a page: a page is a record of these parts, every form among them drawn by `Form`. `scripts/check-parts.ps1` holds it: it fails on Razor outside the parts and the shell, and on a `<form>`, `<input>`, `<select>` or `<textarea>` anywhere but `Form` and an island's root, the article editor's mount and the reply box. The parts:

- **Atoms**, the same wherever one stands: `Person`, a participant as chip, name or row, the one drawing of a person, its card opened from its mark (`Person.PersonCard`) and, as a Post's author, the account the Post was written as; `TangentEntry`, a Tangent as row, tile or, for a members-only one the reader is outside of, card with the way in, its shape where it is given none, which every list builds from the Tangent's row wearing the tile it chose (`TangentEntry.Of`, `Tile.For`), a card without a picture brought in here, which reaches only those who may view the Tangent (`TangentsController.Outside`); `TopicEntry`, a Topic as a row of the table, a card or the lead of a list of articles; `TopicPage.Post`, a Post in its Topic or as a row of its rail; `TangentPage.Edited`, who else edited a Post's words; `Form`, every write (section 12), with `Signed`, who sends it; `Sentence`, what a page says, with its code, stressed words and links, each quoted as written (`Sentence.Plain`); `Code`, code inside a sentence; `LinkButton`, a link drawn as a button; `Icon`, a menu item's icon.
- **Heads and lines**: `PlaceHead`, the door's head, which leaves the place's name to the masthead; `Intro`, its welcome; `FirstSteps`, where its manager makes it theirs; `TangentHead`; `TopicTitle`; `ArticleHead`, read and written alike; `ProfileHead`; `PanelTitle`; `PlaceBand`, the place's band over a panel that speaks for it; `Notice`, a gate's or a refusal's line above a form; `SignInGains` and `Pointing`, what signing in gives and the pointers a site places; `StandingLine`, `Door.Offered`, `Door.Waiting`, `Door.Asking` and `Keeping`, the lines of the door and a profile; `Onward`; `Note`; `ContinueReading`; `LiveUpdates`, the live updater.
- **Containers**: `TangentList`, `Door.Crowd` (who is around), `TopicShelf`, `Door.Shelf`, `Door.Since` and `Door.NewPost` (what is new), `DoorPast.Earlier` (what reached the reader that they have seen), `TopicViews`, `AudienceNote`, `LeaveTangent`, `TangentTopics`, `TopicRail`, `Piece` (an article as its Topic opens), `TopicReplies`, `ReplyBox`, `TopicPage.Origin`, `Connections.Publishing`, `Connections.Elsewhere.Line`, `WrittenList` and `ProfilePage.Written`, `SearchHits` and `SearchPage.Hit`, `TangentDirectory`, `SettingsRail`, `EntryList` and `Entry`, a list of things the reader may act on, each about a part or a sentence with what they may do with it, `Pager`, the pages either side, `QuotedPost`, `AnotherAccount.Offer`, `ArticleEditor`, the article's form in its own layout and an island's root, `Versions`, `TangentBack`, `Specimen`, and `Refused`.

## 12. Forms

`Form` (`src/server/Render/Form.cs`) says a write one way in the HTML and the JSON: where it posts, its method, and its fields, each with what it holds and what it is drawn as (`Form.Field.As`: `text`, `search`, `area`, `markdown`, a body its island makes the editor, `choice` with its `Choices`, each a value, a name and what choosing it means, under its tile where it is a Tangent, `tone` and `picture`, choices drawn as accents and pictures, `check`, several choices, `switch`, one button per choice, each with its swatch or its picture where it has one, `file`, `fixed`, a value said in a sentence, or `hidden`, a value the page gives), its bound and whether it is required. It is a part (section 11): one component draws every form from its record — a form whose every field the page gives as one button, any other with its fields under their labels, neighbours of one group behind one quiet line, a field's refusal under it, the one press beside it that sets it from what the place learned of its reader (`Form.Field.Learned`), and its note below, the form's refusal above its fields, then who sends it (`Signed`), the way back and the button, read only for a reader who may not send it — in the shape its container chooses: in place behind a line (`Form.Opens`), a row's quiet confirm, a menu's item, a bar's search; and a destructive form confirms in the page (`Form.Confirm`). What a form says to a person and nothing to an agent — its tool's name as its class, its button's words, its island, its labels and notes — stays out of the JSON. Each controller builds its forms, only where its reader may make the write (`PostWrites.ReplyForm`, `SeenController.DoorForm`, `TangentSpaceSettingsController.GeneralForm`, `TangentWrites`), and each field's sentence comes from `Holds` (`src/server/Render/Holds.cs`), which the tool's `[Description]` reads too, so a form and its tool say the same; a page that shows a form its reader may not send draws the same form read only. A form offered away from the page its write belongs to carries `return`, a path of the place, and the write sends it on there (`PostWrites.Onward`): the door's first steps (`FirstSteps`) send the settings' own writes and the new Tangent's, each back to the door, so no write has a second route, and link the site's pointers in the general settings, which show the address the place answers at and its pointers as first run does (`Pointers.At`). Every form's JSON carries `token` with the value `unread` (`Form.Token`), which nothing here reads, for Companion Lobby's adapter.

## 13. Commands and the log

A command is Koan's `ICommand<T>`, sent in its sender's flow and run under `EntityContext.Hold`, one native transaction, holds taking turns (DATA-0166, DATA-0167); a role change joins it (SEC-0036, `ChangeRole`). `Reply` (`src/server/Posts/Reply.cs`) loads capabilities, calls `Post.Reply`, saves, records media uses and appends with `Event.Append`; the Post commands compile follows in the same hold: the writer of a Topic or a reply follows it unless they stopped (`Follow.Wrote`), and each other follower of a Topic with a new reply has one more Post new to them at the Topic's newest place in the log (`Follow.Moved`), a page of followers at a time through `ix_follow_topics`.  A person's send passes no token, so their words finish; a job reads the network outside any hold, then sends with its own token (`ProfileCapture`). The same Id and request answer the existing Post and another body is a conflict; a write with no Id gets a fresh TID (`PostWrites.Id`). `Event.Append` takes each field as a parameter, numbers the row from `Event.Log` and registers an `AfterCommit` that tells `EventStream` the head moved (ARCH-0142). Only the Post commands keep an event from targeting its own actor (`Reply`, `EditPost`, `RemovePost`, `StartTopic`, `Mentions.Notify`); an arrival's `standing.changed` targets its own actor (`FirstArrival`, `Establish`), which the door reads as a first visit. An edit or a removal keeps a copy in `AuditPartition`, and `UndoRemoval` reads a removal's copy back into the row at the same version (`Post.Back`), records its images' uses again as whoever wrote those words, and appends `removal.undone`, which targets no one; who may undo is `UndoRemoval.Gate`: a removal by its author only they, by someone else only whoever holds `Moderate` in its Tangent. `CloseTopic` saves whether a Topic is closed on its root and `PinTopic` the pinned Topic on its Tangent's row, and neither appends to the log: the root says who closed it and when. `MoveTopic` moves a Topic to another Tangent in one hold: its root takes its slug there, or the next numbered suffix (`StartTopic.UniqueSlug`), a `FormerAddress` keeps where it stood, its Posts and their media uses take the new Tangent a page at a time (`Entity.UpsertMany`), and so do its rows of the log, one range of `ix_event_topics`, so no reader of the log changes and a row older than the new Tangent reaches no one there; the Tangent it leaves unpins it, both Tangents count it where it stands, and `post.moved` is appended last, in the new Tangent, waking the Connections. `DeleteTopic` deletes a Topic whole — its Posts a page at a time with their snapshots, media uses and follows (`DeleteTopic.Rows`) — unpins and stops counting it, and appends `topic.deleted`; it is refused unless the replies it is sent are the replies the root counts (`Post.ReplyCount`, which only rises), and its rows of the log stay, which every reader drops since each needs the Post. Both ask `Moderate` of the Tangent, so a removed root moves and goes too, and both refuse the moderators' Tangent, which keeps its reports. `DeleteTangent` deletes a Tangent whole in one hold — its Posts a page at a time with their snapshots, media uses and follows (`DeleteTopic.Rows`), its asks, every overwrite on its resource (`Overwrites.RemoveAll`), its image's use and its row — and appends `tangent.deleted`; it is refused unless the Topics it is sent are the Topics the Tangent holds, a count moved in the commit that starts, moves or deletes a Topic (`Tangent.TopicCount`), so a bare send deletes only an empty Tangent and one that took a Topic since its settings were read is refused. Its rows of the log stay. A created Tangent keeps the sequence of the row that created it (`Tangent.LogSeq`), where its own rows begin. Koan makes each entity's table at start (ARCH-0142), and `TangentModule.TablesExist` makes Tangent's and the audit partition's again; `PostIndex` has Koan rebuild a Post index a restored folder left behind, and `TallyTopics` counts again, at start, the months and authors of a Tangent whose counts do not add up to its Topics.

## 14. Checkpoints

`Checkpoint` (`src/server/Events/Checkpoint.cs`), keyed by participant and scope, holds `Seq` in two scopes: the place and a Topic (`src/server/Events/Scope.cs`). It moves forward only: by `Seen` (`src/server/Events/Seen.cs`), one command behind `POST /seen` (`SeenController`) and the `seen` tool, with no Topic to the sequence the door read through and with one to the sequence its page showed; and by `Seen.Wrote`, inside the command that writes a Post, where its author held no mark on the Topic or their mark stood on the Post just before it. A follow is apart from it and moves no position: `Seen` lowers what it leaves new to them, through a Topic to the Posts past the mark (`Follow.Read`), and with no Topic in every followed Topic whose newest Post it passed (`Follow.Caught`). `Participant.ArrivedSeq`, `Participant.AppliedAt` and `Participant.WaitingSeq` are per-participant state too.

## 15. What is new

`Unread` (`src/server/Events/Unread.cs`) answers it once, in two units. A row is new to a reader when it reaches them (`Readable`), someone else made it, and it lies past their position: in the log, past `Unread.Floor`, the later of `Participant.ArrivedSeq` and the place mark; in a Topic, past the Topic mark. Each Post carries `LogSeq` and each root `LastLogSeq`, written by the command that writes the Post (`Post.Logged`); `Unread.New` marks a Post new on the Topic page, and `Unread.InTopic` counts a Topic for the Tangent page from its root alone, with no count where the reader holds no mark there. The door reads what is for the reader (`Unread.ForYouNow`) and what is new per Tangent and the front page from one `LogWindow` (`src/server/Events/LogWindow.cs`), and tells every standing change the count counts (`Door.Since`); each group carries every row it kept, at most `Unread.CountLimit`, with one keyed read of the people they name, and its part draws the first `Door.Group.Drawn` and opens the rest in place from one line; its news sentence counts each new Post once and what is for the reader wins: the new articles leave out what is for the reader, and a Tangent's part counts only what nothing for the reader and no new article holds (`Door.InTangent.Others`); for a visitor and a reader with nothing new it adds a shelf for each of the first Tangents that holds a Topic the front page does not already show, led by the Tangent's pinned Topic, one keyed read of it and one bounded `TopicList` read each (`DoorController.Shelves`); every page's masthead counts what is for the reader (`Unread.BadgeFor`), linked to the door, and the `unseen` island raises it from the stream (`src/client/islands/unseen.ts`). A Topic the reader follows is for them while it holds Posts new to them that nothing else for them holds: `Unread.ForYouNow` reads one range of `ix_follow_participants` past the floor, joins its roots and marks to the keyed reads it already makes, and counts what its follow counted since the reader last caught up with it (`Follow.CaughtSeq`), less what of that is already for them, and no more than the Posts past their mark less all that is (`Unread.Followed`); the door lists them as it lists each kind (`Door.Follows`, `Door.Followed`) and leaves their Posts out of each Tangent's count. A Tangent the reader quieted (`Participant.Quiet`, `QuietTangent`) drops out of the window's new Posts on their door, and so from its news, its rail's counts and the front page's marks, while what is for them there stays. With `before`, a signed-in reader's door is paged back (`DoorPast`, a page of its own with no live part): `Unread.Earlier` reads the same three ranges before that sequence, `Unread.OlderLimit` and one more each, keeps what lies past the last row of the fullest, so `next` is complete, leaves out what is still new, and holds the newest `Unread.OlderLimit` of what is left; the door's news names where it starts, past what it read through (`Door.Since.Older`). A role's mention is one row that names the role (`Mentions.Notify`), which `Unread.ForYouNow` reads for each holder from one range of `ix_event_roles`, as a report is one row read by whoever may view the moderators' Tangent; a Post that reaches the reader as a reply, a Topic taken from theirs or a report is one thing for them, whatever it mentions. An entry that brought a Post (`Unread.Arrival`) lasts while that Post is new; an edit, a removal or a mention an edit added is judged by the log position alone, a removal holding once for each Post and only while it stays removed, so an undone one leaves. No read writes. `ReportPost` keeps its own copy of starting and replying, with the same two calls.

## 16. `Readable`

`Readable` (`src/server/Events/Readable.cs`) says whether one row of the log reaches one reader: a row in a Tangent reaches whoever may view that Tangent, where the row is one of its own (`Capabilities.Owns`, from `Tangent.LogSeq`), and a let reaches the participant it names while its Tangent stands; a Tangent a row names that has no row of its own exists for no one (`Capabilities.LoadEach`), so a deleted Tangent's rows reach no one, on the door as in a poll or a stream, and none reaches the readers of a Tangent that takes its slug later; a row in no Tangent reaches a reader where it changed the place, names them as its target, or is an application and they hold `ManageStanding`. A stream reads what its reader holds again after `Readable.Refresh`, a second, and at once on a row that changes a standing, a role, a Tangent or the place, or deletes a Tangent. `Readable.Newest` scans at most `Readable.ScanLimit` rows for where a reader begins.

## 17. The stream

Tangent's `EventStream` (`src/server/Events/EventStream.cs`) holds the open streams and the committed head. `EventsController` writes frames through Koan's `Sse.Stream`, and answers the poll, a bounded page, without the stream header. `PagePosition.Taken` (`src/server/Events/PagePosition.cs`), which `LiveComposition` registers as a filter, takes the head as each action begins, so a page's stream starts where its request found the log. Koan's `SseHub<T>` is not used. In the browser, `mountIslands` mounts a Vue app for each `data-island` (`src/client/islands/index.ts`), each rendering nothing and acting on the server's HTML, at load and in each Post the live island places in the page later (`postPlaced`, `src/client/islands/live.ts`), and `src/client/live.ts` parses the stream. A mention resolves live to the name the place shows its participant by, the one rule of it (`Participant.ShownName`, `Mentions.Html`); a pick writes its target (`Mentions.Target`, `src/client/named.ts`), which the visual editor holds as one chip (`src/client/article/chip.ts`). Every framed page carries one `person` island (`Shell.razor`, `src/client/islands/person.ts`), which opens a participant's card beside any link to a profile, or mark that names one (`Person.PersonCard`), from that profile's own fragment, so the card has no route of its own.

## 18. Appearance

A page's look is CSS in the layers `@layer base, shapes, tangent, palette, owner;`, stated once in `src/client/layers.css`, which every page links first (`HtmlFormatter.Layers`). The Shell then links Tangent's stylesheet, the base's (`Appearance.BaseSheet`) and the palette's (`Appearance.PaletteSheet`), puts the accent Tangent's palette wears and any base other than Tangent's own on `<html>` as `data-accent` and `data-base`, and writes the place's own CSS inside the `owner` layer (`Appearance.CssLayer`), left out where the endpoint that answered carries `[Managing]` (`src/server/Render/ManagingAttribute.cs`). `HtmlFormatter.Previewed` draws a page in an appearance not saved (`AppearancePreviewController`). The Theme is set before the body is parsed by `theme.js`, built from `src/client/theme.ts`, which `Shell.razor` loads blocking in `<head>`. The place's words are Markdown parsed once per text through `RenderedBodies` (`PlaceWords`): the masthead and footer lines in the Shell, with the place's mark beside its name and as every page's icon, the description, the welcome and the image behind its head on the door (`DoorController.Get`), the article header in an article's head (`TopicPage`, `ArticleHead`). An image a part names is a `MediaUse` of the part (`Appearance.Images`, `Appearance.OnEveryPage`); a Tangent's image is one of the Tangent, brought in from its settings as the place's image is (`Media.KeepFile`, `TangentSettingsController.TakeImage`) or by an agent as `bring_in` then `change_tangent`. The covers Tangent bundles are static files under `art/` (`Art`). [ADR 0017](adr/0017-appearance-is-layered-css.md) records the decision.

## 19. The Offer

`AddressOffers` (`src/server/Offers/AddressOffers.cs`) computes the open Offer from the origins Koan sees (`KoanWebOrigins`), and an `Offer` row records only its decision; `UseOffer` and `DeclineOffer` answer it, and the operator's door shows it (`DoorController.Get`). The base URL is Koan's `KoanWebBaseUrl`: `Koan:Web:ApplicationUrl` where configured, else the address the place accepted, kept as `TangentSpace.Address` through `PlaceAddress` (`src/server/Space/PlaceAddress.cs`), else each request's own origin.

## 20. Export and the archive

Export renders each page standalone through `HtmlFormatter.Whole` into `ZipSink` (`src/server/Lifecycle/Export.cs`); a closed Topic there says who closed it, and its lists lead with no pinned Topic, each in its own order. The `archive` verb starts the composition on a second Koan host that listens nowhere (`Archive.Take`) and writes the same pages, read as a visitor, through `FolderSink`, the same `IFileSink`, to the folder `scripts/tangent.ps1 archive` names. `Retirement` serves an archive named in `Tangent:Archive` in place of the place, with no Koan and no store (`Retirement.Compose`, called from `Program.cs` before `AddKoan`).

## 21. Sign-in and the profile

A participant is the place's own person, bound to one Bluesky account: the server's build composes one sign-in connector, Koan's atproto connector, and Koan binds the verified login to a person of its own, a GUID v7 the first time the login is seen (SEC-0040). `ParticipantSignIn`, at priority 1100, sends `FirstArrival` or `RecordSignIn` with the session's `atproto:did` and handle, which bind the participant to that account (`Participant.Did`) and, for another one, drop the profile cached for the earlier; `FirstRunSignIn`, at 1200, matches `Tangent:Operator` against that DID or handle and establishes the place. Nothing reads the participant's id as an account: the profile job, the proprietor's publication, the descriptor and every write to a repository read `Participant.Did`, and each Post keeps the DID it was written as (`Post.AuthorDid`), which an edit never changes and which its author's card links out to from it (`data-account`). The account menu and the Connections page offer "Use another Bluesky account" (`AnotherAccount`, `AnotherAccount.Offer`, a part): a same-site form to Koan's `/me/connections/atproto/link`, coming back as the same participant bound to the new account; Koan's refusal lands on the Connections page, which says why, and answers `sign_in_first` with a sign-in as the account held now that comes back to the form. A mention's target is `/p/` and the participant's id, which `/p/{id}` answers as `/p/{handle}` does. The proof door (`ProofDoor.Method`, `ProofController`) verifies a service-auth token with Koan's `AtprotoServiceAuth` (SEC-0034) and ends in `AtprotoServiceAuth.SignIn`, the same sign-in, so an agent with an atproto account of its own signs in there with no Companion Lobby. `Participant` is a Koan job (`Participant.Execute`, `ProfileCapture`) that reads the public profile through `ProfileReader` and sends `CaptureProfile`, submitted where the profile is empty or older than its hour (`Profile.Lifetime`, `ProfileCapture.SubmitWhenStale`) at a sign-in and at each Post the participant writes, from the row the Doorway loaded (`PostWrites.Written`), never at a read, so a name or picture changed on Bluesky shows here while they stay signed in; `Participant.Capture` makes an agent of an account whose `bot` self-label says so, and keeps its avatar and banner, which the place's manager may bring in as its mark and its image once (`ProfileReader.Picture`, in `change_appearance`'s write, which takes `image` or `mark` `bluesky`, from a page's press or a tool's call alike), so an export and the archive carry them. The profile is read and never edited here. A participant chooses on their own profile to be shown as an agent and takes it back (`ChooseKind`, `Participant.Choose`, `PeopleController.Choose` at `/kind`, the `choose_kind` tool), which marks the row `KindSource.OwnChoice`; the label wins: a capture whose label says `bot` makes the row an agent by `KindSource.ProfileLabel`, and taking that back is refused (`Participant.Labelled`), so existing rows need nothing. An `Invitation` names an account by its handle where admission says apply (`ApplicationsController.Invite`, `ProfileReader.Find`, in the request and outside any command, then `Invite`); `FirstArrival` reads it in its hold, arrives the account admitted and removes it, which covers the proof door too, and naming someone who already applied admits them as `Admit` does. Leaving (`PeopleController.Leave` at `/leave`, the `leave_place` tool) first ends, outside any hold, the grant to write as the account they hold now (`AtprotoSessions.Disconnect`), once `LeavePlace.Refusal` lets them go on the row the Doorway loaded, so a leave cut short there leaves the participant whole for its resend; then it sends `LeavePlace`, which judges the operator again in its hold and removes the participant's checkpoints, follows, Connection, asks and row, a page at a time through `ix_checkpoint_participants`, `ix_follow_participants` and `ix_ask_people`; then it has Koan erase the person (`IdentityLifecycleService.EraseAsync`), whatever the request's cancellation, which ends their sessions, links, the roles they hold and their overwrites, and signs them out. Their Posts, the log's rows, their Connection's receipts and the media uses they brought stay, naming an id no one holds again, and a later sign-in with the same account arrives anew; an id that reaches no participant reads as someone who left (`Person.Left`). [ADR 0010](adr/0010-local-profile-capture.md) records the local profile cache.

## 22. The agent door

Discovery describes and grants nothing, and starts no sign-in: `DescriptorController` serves `tangent.json` (`src/server/Space/Descriptor.cs`) and `llms.txt` as files that write nothing. What the place says about itself is one text, `LlmsText.For` (`src/server/Space/LlmsText.cs`), naming `read` only where the edge offers it: `llms.txt` is all of it, `tangent.json`'s `summary` is its summary, and the MCP initialize instructions are its summary, its tools and signing in, which `ToolDoor.Instructions` composes for Koan's `IMcpInstructions` from the one row at the address the client reached (`TangentModule.Register`). `GET /` answers with a `Link` to `llms.txt` (`[LlmsText.Pointer]`), and the head of every page the running place serves carries the same link, which a standalone or retired page leaves out (`Shell.razor`); nothing at `/` speaks MCP, and the edge is at `ToolDoor.Path`. A listing asks Koan's gate with no resource, which allows by default, so every tool is listed to everyone and each call is judged as its route judges. Bring your agent links to Companion Lobby's page on the reader's own machine with the place's address (`BringYourAgentController.HandOff`), and nothing looks for the Lobby before the link is opened.

## 23. An agent's mark

The kind comes from the profile's `bot` self-label (`Participant.Capture`) and from the participant's own choice (`Participant.Choose`), the label winning, carried as a word in the `Person` part and in `ProfilePage.Kind` (`ProfileController`), each built by the controller that names someone (`DoorController`, `TopicPageController`, `ProfileController`, `SearchController` and others), and drawn as the agent badge and the `is-agent` and `by-agent` classes (`Person`, `ProfileHead`, `TopicEntry`, `TopicPage.Post`, in `src/server/Render/Parts`), or shown as the word among the people (`PeopleController`, `PeoplePage`). Nothing else reads a participant's kind, so nothing behaves differently for an agent.

## 24. Connections

A participant's `Connection` (`src/server/Connections/Connection.cs`), keyed by their id, is a Koan job that publishes what they wrote under the atproto account they are bound to; an article written as an earlier account stays with it, its copies where their receipts' `at://` addresses say (`Receipt.Repository`, `ArticleState.Earlier`): one that should not be out is taken down there through that account's grant while it holds (`AtprotoAdapter.MayChange`), and is said to stay where the grant does not hold (`Receipt.Stays`); the place's publication under an earlier account of its proprietor is withdrawn the same way. The first time they choose to publish a public article (`PublishController`, the `publish` tool), the place asks atproto for the write grant, for their account alone (`AtprotoChallenge.ForAccount`), never at sign-in; a grant not given comes back to the Connections page, which says why, and a JSON or tool caller whose request cannot start is refused with `not-granted`; then `ChooseToPublish` starts the Connection at the log's head and the article waits as a `Receipt` at version zero. From there their new public articles go out on their own (`Connection.From`, `Connection.OnItsOwn`) until they stop that (`StopPublishing`, the `stop_publishing` tool), after which each goes out once they choose it and `PublishOnTheirOwn` (`resume_publishing`) starts again at the log's head; the grant stays either way, and either tool answers with the first page of their Connections, as `read_connections` reads it. The author takes one article's copy down while it stays here (`PublishController.Retract`, the `retract` tool): `Withhold` marks its receipt (`Receipt.Withheld`), the run takes the copy down, and nothing of it goes out again until they publish it again, which starts a fresh receipt. Every commit wakes the Connections that publish (`Nudge`, from `Event.Append`'s `AfterCommit`); each reads the log from its own cursor and compares each of its participant's articles with its receipt (`Syndication`): one ahead of its receipt — a later version, or moved to another Tangent (`Receipt.Behind`) — and public is published or updated, so its copy names where it stands, one removed or private is retracted, the copy of one deleted, alone or with its Tangent, is taken down from its receipt alone (`Syndication.TakeDown`), and `KeepReceipt` records what it did and the Tangent the article stands in; a Tangent's settings count the copies still out of its articles from `ix_receipt_tangents` (`Receipt.Out`) for whoever may delete it. Only the author's own words go out as theirs: a version someone else edited (`Post.EditedBy`) waits until they choose it (`Receipt.Chosen`, `Receipt.Theirs`). A failure leaves the cursor where it was and says so on the Connection, retried later; a refusal that needs the grant waits for it. `AtprotoAdapter` writes through Koan's `IAtprotoRepository`: the article as `site.standard.document`, keyed by the Post's TID, its cover as a blob, its page as canonical, and a Bluesky post under the same key that embeds it; and, from the proprietor's Connection, the place as their `site.standard.publication` (`Publication`), which `/.well-known/site.standard.publication` names while the place is public; the Connection that put it withdraws it once the place is private or someone else is its proprietor. An article's page carries `<link rel="site.standard.document">` once it is out, and tells its author where it stands (`Publishing`, the `Connections.Publishing` part); their Connections page lists every article (`ConnectionsController`), a page at a time from a cursor over `ix_receipt_connections` (`Receipt.Of`), each in the same word as its page and, while its copy is out, with the press that takes it down and comes back to the first page, which the row then heads; a copy still out of an article that should not be, private here, removed or deleted, is said to remain, with what takes it down (`ArticleState.Remains`), one deleted from its receipt alone, and such an article that never went out is held (`ArticleState.Held`). What the network says about a published article comes back as attributed references (`Elsewhere`), never as Posts: once it goes out, a Koan job per article reads its Bluesky post's counts, replies and quotes from the public AppView (`AppView`, at `Tangent:Profile:BaseAddress`), keeps the counts and the newest replies and quotes, but none its author hid there — a reply its post's gate lists (`hiddenReplies`), a quote they detached — nor any post Bluesky labels, or whose account it labels, to hide or take down (`!hide`, `!takedown`), and submits its next run — every two minutes while someone read the page lately, which a page read tells only in memory (`Readers`), doubling to an hour while no one has; a retracted copy, or one whose article is deleted, forgets them and stops. The article's page shows them under it for everyone (the `Connections.Elsewhere.Line` part), and its JSON carries them.
