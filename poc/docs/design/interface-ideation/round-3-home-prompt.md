# Forum foundation — home

Built-in imagegen. Structural references: live Waterhole feed and NodeBB thread screenshots inspected in the browser. Speculative Tangent design; no app implementation.

Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate ONE highly polished full-size desktop screenshot of Tangent Space's forum HOME, based on the two reference screenshots.
Input references: image 1 is Waterhole's compact discussion feed; image 2 is NodeBB's long-thread shell. Use them as STRUCTURAL REFERENCES for a familiar capable forum. Do not copy their logos, content, arbitrary demo colors, metrics clutter or exact branding. The user wants this established forum format with excellent visual design, not experimental chat/map/tree metaphors.

Design: warm bone outer canvas (#F5F3EF), near-white opaque content surfaces, deep plum-ink typography, restrained luminous violet primary action and pale lavender selected states. A confident contemporary sans-serif system, semibold readable Topic titles, carefully spaced secondary text, precise fine dividers, 8-12px radii and almost no shadow. This should feel like a beautifully crafted daily-use forum. Natural community artwork supplies personality. No huge serif marketing headline, no wallpaper, no glassmorphism, no floating 3D mockup. Flat straight-on actual app viewport, landscape, highest detail and crisp readable text. No presentation board labels or device frame.

Layout:
1. Slim full-width app header: Tangent Space wordmark at left, a genuinely useful wide Search conversations field in the center, small Mara account avatar/name right.
2. One consistent 230px left sidebar under header, with simple line icons and visible labels: Conversations (selected), Recent, Explore Tangents. Then section label TANGENTS. Three generous but compact community entries, each a 52px photographic crop next to its name and one-line purpose:
Night Garden — Small observations, bigger questions. Photograph of a botanical conservatory at dusk warmly lit amid lush foliage.
The Workshop — Make, repair, share. Photograph of a sunlit wooden workbench.
Far Horizons — Places worth returning to. Photograph of a misty coastal headland.
These entries retain the user's image-per-Tangent idea without requiring a card-grid detour. Avoid an additional far-left icon rail or a second sidebar. At bottom a quiet Create a Tangent text action and About this Space.
3. Main region broad and dominant. Compact heading "Conversations" and secondary "From across this Space". To the right a violet "Start a conversation" button. No giant welcome banner.
4. A slim toolbar just below: "Latest" selected, "New", "Unanswered"; right-side small "All Tangents" scope selector. This is recognizably Waterhole-like, scannable forum navigation.
5. ONE clean flat discussion-list surface with 7 useful rows, NOT individual floating cards. Each row has author avatar at left; strong readable title; one discreet community label, original author and latest-reply information underneath; a short one-line excerpt where useful; a consistently aligned reply count at right. Name original authors and recent participants so the forum feels inhabited. Fine row dividers; generous enough to read but compact enough to show seven discussions at once.

No decorative Topic thumbnails: images identify COMMUNITIES, author avatars identify Posts. No voting columns, no giant view counts, no online-statistics sidebar, no leaderboard, no sidebar manifesto. This is a functional community product, not a dashboard or magazine. Preserve the well-known forum list hierarchy from Waterhole and NodeBB while noticeably improving typography, rhythm, image identity and warmth. Do not introduce chat bubbles, branch diagrams, canvas maps, multiple composers or a large community-card grid.
