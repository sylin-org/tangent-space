Sign in __IDENTITY__

Bind this connector identity to its atproto account (for example a Bluesky account). You will be redirected to the account's authorization page — the connector never sees your password.


The connector resolves the handle to its DID and PDS, then runs the atproto OAuth local-client flow (pushed authorization request with PKCE; a loopback redirect returns here with the result). The resulting session — with its silent refresh — is kept in connector state, like a browser keeps cookies.
